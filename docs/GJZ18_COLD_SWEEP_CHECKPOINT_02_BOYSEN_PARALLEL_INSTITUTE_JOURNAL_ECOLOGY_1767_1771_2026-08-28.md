# GJZ18 cold sweep checkpoint 02: Boysen and the parallel historical-journal ecology, 1767–1771

Date: 2026-08-28  
Status: CONTROLLED INFRASTRUCTURAL LEAD — NOT A POLEMICAL OPPONENT

## Result

Friedrich Eberhard Boysen should **not** currently be promoted as a cold controversy opponent of Johann Christoph Gatterer. He is more useful as evidence that the Göttingen historical-institute programme was internally distributed across parallel publication infrastructures almost from the moment Gatterer's `Allgemeine historische Bibliothek` appeared.

The key fact pattern is unusually dense:

- Boysen was an **ordinary member of the Königliches Institut der historischen Wissenschaften zu Göttingen**;
- he founded/edited the `Allgemeines historisches Magazin` (Halle: Johann Justinus Gebauer), 1767–1770;
- Gatterer's `Allgemeine historische Bibliothek von Mitgliedern des königlichen Instituts der historischen Wissenschaften zu Göttingen` was likewise published by Johann Justinus Gebauer in Halle, 1767–1771;
- Boysen simultaneously edited the `Allgemeine Welthistorie ... in einem vollständigen und pragmatischen Auszuge`; the bibliographic record identifies the first volume as issued **with a preface by Johann Christoph Gatterer**;
- external learned journals reviewed Boysen's `Allgemeines historisches Magazin` and Gatterer's `Allgemeine historische Bibliothek` in the same review ecology, sometimes in strikingly alternating sequence.

The safest interpretation is therefore not `Boysen versus Gatterer`, but:

> **the historical institute's jurisdiction was never embodied in a single journal or a single authorial voice. A member of the institute could run a parallel historical magazine through the same Halle publisher while collaborating with Gatterer in the universal-history publishing programme.**

This matters for the Clio-to-controversy project because later fragmentation/competition among historical journals should not be narrated as a sudden post-Schlözer event. A multi-centred print infrastructure existed inside the institute's own ecology by 1767.

---

## 1. Boysen's institutional status is primary-bibliographically explicit

The title formula of `Allgemeines historisches Magazin` identifies Boysen as:

`des königlichen Instituts der historischen Wissenschaften zu Göttingen ordentlichem Mitgliede`.

The Gebauer publishing bibliography controls the series as six issues/stücke, Halle, 1767–1770.

Evidence: `PRIMARY-BIBLIOGRAPHIC / PUBLISHER-CATALOGUE CONTROLLED`.

---

## 2. Same publisher, same years, two historical periodical infrastructures

Gebauer's publishing bibliography places, in the same 1769 production list:

- Boysen, `Allgemeines historisches Magazin`, fifth issue;
- Gatterer (ed.), `Allgemeine historische Bibliothek`, vols. 9–12.

Both are printed/published by Johann Justinus Gebauer in Halle.

This establishes a concrete shared production infrastructure, not merely thematic proximity.

A useful model is:

`Königliches historisches Institut`
→ `Gatterer / AHB`
+
`Boysen / Allgemeines historisches Magazin`
→ `same Halle publisher / overlapping historical market`.

Do not infer editorial rivalry without letters/prefaces saying so.

---

## 3. External review ecology treats the two periodicals as adjacent products

GJZ18 exposes an especially suggestive sequence in the Erlangen `Compendium historiae litterariae novissimae`:

- 1767: Boysen, `Allgemeines historisches Magazin`, St. 1;
- 1768: Gatterer, `Allgemeine historische Bibliothek`, Bd. 6;
- 1769: Boysen, `Allgemeines historisches Magazin`, St. 3;
- 1769: Gatterer, `Allgemeine historische Bibliothek`, Bd. 9–10;
- 1771: Gatterer, `Allgemeine historische Bibliothek`, Bd. 14;
- 1771: Boysen, `Allgemeines historisches Magazin`, St. 6.

The GJZ18 result shown for this sequence is neutral-rated.

Evidence: `GJZ18-INDEX CONTROLLED`.

The important point is not evaluation polarity but market legibility: contemporaries could encounter the two journals as neighboring specialist-historical organs.

---

## 4. Boysen and Gatterer also collaborate in the universal-history project

Dikon/GVK bibliographic records preserve a title form for Boysen's `Allgemeine Welthistorie ... in einem vollständigen und pragmatischen Auszuge`:

`Mit einer Vorrede von Joh. Christoph Gatterers herausgegeben von D. Friedrich Eberhard Boysen ...`

GJZ18 independently records several 1767 reviews of vol. 1 with **Boysen and Gatterer both indexed as involved persons**.

Thus any rivalry hypothesis must reckon with direct collaboration.

Safe status:

`COLLABORATIVE / PARALLEL INFRASTRUCTURE`, not `DIRECT_POLEMIC`.

---

## 5. Gebauer archive metadata confirms that contemporaries grouped these print projects together

The Gebauer-Schwetschke publisher archive in Kalliope contains multiple 1768 correspondence records whose topical brackets place Boysen's magazine and Gatterer's AHB side by side, for example:

- Johann Samuel Heinsius, Leipzig, 5 Aug. 1768: `[Klotz: Deutsche Bibliothek ...; Boysen: Allgemeines Historisches Magazin; Gatterer: Allgemeine Historische Bibliothek]`, shelfmark `A 6.2.6 Nr. 11490`;
- Anton Ferdinand Röse, Greifswald, 25 Sept. 1768: includes both Boysen's magazine and Gatterer's AHB, shelfmark `A 6.2.6 Nr. 11663`;
- Johann Ludwig Gese, Köthen, 18 Nov. 1768: brackets include the `Allgemeine Welthistorie`, Boysen's magazine and Gatterer's AHB, shelfmark `A 6.2.6 Nr. 9836`.

These catalogue brackets do **not** reveal content or conflict. They do show that in the publisher's correspondence ecology the projects were materially entangled.

Evidence: `ARCHIVAL METADATA CONTROLLED`.

---

## 6. Consequence for the larger controversy model

This checkpoint changes the model in one important way.

Do not assume:

`one Göttingen historical programme`
→ `one Gatterer journal`
→ `external challengers later appear`.

A better model is:

`institutional programme`
→ `multiple internal/affiliated publication channels`
→ `external review comparison`
→ `later explicit methodological and professional contests`.

This makes `jurisdiction` a network property rather than Gatterer's private possession from the outset.

It also sharpens what counts as an actual cold opponent:

- Boysen: **not yet**;
- Hausen: stronger, because review + review-indexed correspondence exists;
- anonymous Hamburg reviewer: critical reception only;
- Auserlesene Bibliothek reviewers: unresolved external evaluation surface;
- Meusel / later journals: possible competitive infrastructure requiring programme comparison.
