### B. Blumenbach 1795 is not heredity-empty

Primary Latin §§39–40 close the following points:

- artificial or accidental modifications repeated through long generations are explicitly considered as a possible route toward congenital conformation;
- Blumenbach remains undecided rather than affirming such conversion as a doctrine;
- familial / `gentilitial` characters can propagate through more or fewer generations with greater or lesser constancy;
- several causes can reinforce or oppose one another;
- organic bodies/organs differ in susceptibility and resistance (`pronitas / renisus`);
- causal action may be more immediate or more mediated;
- persistence is not assumed to behave symmetrically under opposite environmental transfers;
- stable cranial forms and eye colours are associated with more mediated and partly unknown causes.

Therefore the 1795 model is:

`variable propagation + variable constancy + causal pluralism + organ/trait-specific susceptibility / persistence`.

It is **not yet** the 1797 binary gate:

`necessary inheritance → Rasse / lack of necessity → Spielart`.

The strongest transition is:

`variable propagation phenomenon → necessary reproductive persistence promoted to category gate`.
