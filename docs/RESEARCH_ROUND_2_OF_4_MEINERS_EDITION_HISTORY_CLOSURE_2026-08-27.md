## 1. 1785 p.23 ↔ 1793 p.23 — status corrected again

Earlier repo work temporarily suspected that the anti-single-body-part rule might belong only to the 1793 second edition because a recent specialist volume quoted the rule with a 1793 p.23 locator.

That suspicion is now materially weakened.

Two independent modern controls explicitly point to the **1785 first edition, pp.23–24** as a relevant source for Meiners's treatment of bodily evidence:

- Bruce Buchan and Linda Andersson Burnett, `Specimens for the “science of man”: Skulls, race, and instructions in James Prichard’s Enlightenment ethnology` (History of Science, 2025), note 85, cites Meiners, *Grundriss der Geschichte der Menschheit* (1785), pp.23–24 in its discussion of the bodily/racial evidentiary tradition inherited by Robert Jameson.
- The 2023 Meiners specialist volume independently uses *Grundriß* material at pp.23–24 for Meiners's programmatic definition of the history of humankind; this confirms the first-edition page locus is real and structurally central, although it does not by itself reproduce the exact anti-single-trait sentence.

Current status:

`1785 p.23–24 relevance = exact-page modern control`

`1785 exact anti-single-trait wording = not yet primary-direct`

`1793 p.23 exact wording = specialist quotation control`

Do **not** retain the old working assumption `anti-single-trait rule may first appear in 1793`.

The likely chronology is now:

`rule already present in 1785 or immediately adjacent first-edition text`
→ `publicly reiterated in 1790 skull/provenance controversy`
→ `retained/recast in 1793 second edition`.

Primary page comparison is still required before declaring verbatim continuity.

## 2. 1793 p.60 — Kant uptake status

Robert Bernasconi's 2025 chapter in *Colonialism and Enlightenment* gives exact-page control:

- Meiners, *Grundriss* 1793, p.60 explicitly cites Kant's 1785 essay on race;
- Bernasconi reads the passage as implying that at least some difference between Meiners and Kant was terminological;
- pp.47 and 59–60 support the related proposition that original unity of the human species could be combined with essential racial difference.

Current status:

`1793 p.60 Kant citation = exact-page specialist control + primary carrier fixed`

`literal primary wording = pending direct page inspection`

Interpretive stop rule:

`explicit Kant citation ≠ wholesale Kantian adoption`.

The target is to establish what operation Meiners accepts, rejects or redescribes, not to infer doctrinal dependence from citation alone.

## 3. 1785 p.39 ↔ 1793 p.85 — climate/descent revision status

Bernasconi gives exact-page comparison:

- 1785 first edition p.39;
- 1793 second edition p.85;
- the later edition deletes a climate reference in order to foreground `Abstammung`.

Current status:

`edition-level revision = exact-page specialist control`

`literal 1785 and 1793 wording = pending direct page inspection`

This fits the already established 1790–1792 causal history only if formulated narrowly:

`increased weighting of descent in a specific revised passage`

not

`global replacement of climate by heredity`.

The 1790 and 1792 primary evidence already falsifies the stronger global-replacement model.
