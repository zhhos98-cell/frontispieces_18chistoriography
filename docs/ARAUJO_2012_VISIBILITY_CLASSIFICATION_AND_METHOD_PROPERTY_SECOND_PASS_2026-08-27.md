# Araújo 2012 — second pass: visibility, classification, and method-property

Date: 2026-08-27
Status: active secondary-control synthesis

## 1. Purpose

This pass treats André de Melo Araújo, *Weltgeschichte in Göttingen. Eine Studie über das spätaufklärerische universalhistorische Denken, 1756–1815* (2012), not as background reading but as a secondary control and primary-source locator for the active colour-as-method branch.

The book is especially valuable because it connects three questions currently under investigation:

1. how Göttingen universal history made relations among dispersed historical parts perceptible;
2. how Gatterer and Schlözer differed in the epistemological organisation of universal history;
3. how visible bodily difference, especially skin colour, could be proposed and then demoted as an organising principle.

## 2. Historiographical form as a technology of making relations visible

Araújo repeatedly frames late-Enlightenment universal history as an attempt to make relations among fragmented historical parts perceptible.

His introductory formulation describes the problem as grasping the becoming of history in its fragmented parts through now-perceptible relations and making them into a compatible whole by means of a historiographical form.

This is important for the colour project because it independently establishes a historiographical field in which `sichtbar`, `wahrnehmbar`, relation, form and whole are already central analytical terms.

The relevant project consequence is:

`historical relation`
→ `historiographical form`
→ `increased perceptibility / visibility`

Colour can therefore be analysed as one operator within a larger representational programme rather than as an isolated decorative technology.

## 3. Gatterer: making relations sharper

Araújo describes Gatterer's `vierfache Beschäftigung` of universal history as extending Göttingen historiography through an ethnographically oriented view of individual humans, all humans together, private life and the great world. The point of this expansion is that parts can be made more sharply visible in their historical relations to one another.

This gives a useful secondary control for the operator model:

`expanded empirical object field`
→ `relations among parts`
→ `sharper visibility`

The visual problem is therefore downstream of the empirical extension of the object field.

## 4. Gatterer versus Schlözer: empirical object versus methodological plan

Araújo's chapter `Innovation und Federkrieg`, especially the section on the epistemological background of the 1773 polemic, explicitly argues that the conflict cannot be reduced to money, students, prestige or personal rivalry.

The polemic also reveals competing epistemologically valid approaches to representing universal-historical events in Zusammenhang.

Araújo's reconstruction differentiates the two programmes as follows:

### Gatterer

- emphasis on the empirical object-field;
- `vierfache Beschäftigung` of universal history;
- expansion and articulation of what belongs to the historical object.

### Schlözer

- emphasis on method;
- four `Methoden von Weltgeschichte`;
- methodical Plan for representation and operationalisation of a principle by which parts can be explained in Zusammenhang.

This is a strong prior-art guard. The project must not claim novelty for discovering that the Gatterer–Schlözer quarrel had substantive epistemological content.

The remaining project-specific question is narrower:

> Did graphical, tabular, synchronistic or chromatic implementation form part of the `Plan / Methode / Denkungsart / Entdeckungen` that could be claimed as intellectual property?

## 5. Primary loci supplied by Araújo

Araújo gives several primary loci that should replace broad blind-searching:

- Gatterer, *Antwort auf die Schlözersche Species Facti*, especially the `Plan und Probe` framing and later defence of priority;
- Schlözer, *Species Facti*, especially the pages on `Erfindungen`, `Aenderungen`, `Plündernde` and `Geplünderte`;
- Gatterer, *Abriß der Universalhistorie in ihrem ganzen Umfange* (1773), cited by Araújo in the method comparison;
- Gatterer's earlier handbooks for the relation between chronological and synchronistic methods.

Next primary control should therefore focus on whether `Plan` includes implementation rather than merely conceptual content.

## 6. Synchrony and chronology as representation operators

Araújo reconstructs Gatterer's recurring problem as the connection of synchronous and chronological forms of historical Darstellung.

An early formulation requires:

- chronological narration of notable events of each people/state to show state changes;
- simultaneous narration of all realms and states flourishing at one time in a synchronistic Zusammenhang.

The important point for the colour branch is that `synchronistic relation` is already constructed by a representational operator before pigment enters.

Thus:

`chronological sequences`
+
`synchronous co-presence`
→ `historical relation-field`
→ possible later graphical/chromatic enhancement.

## 7. Hautfarbe as a failed or demoted organising principle

Araújo gives a particularly important colour-method control.

Gatterer considered bodily difference, including `Gestalt` and `Hautfarbe`, in the context of Anthropographie / Geographie der Menschenkörper. Yet Araújo emphasises that Gatterer ultimately refrained from making such accidental bodily distinctions into an organising principle of his geographical and historical writings.

The distinction is epistemically strong:

`visible bodily property`
≠
`essential difference`
≠
`historically adequate organising principle`.

This means that literal colour can be perfectly observable and still fail as an authorised historical classifier.

## 8. Skin colour and language: evidentiary demotion

Araújo's later reconstruction suggests a trajectory in which language becomes a stronger indicator of population origin than skin colour.

The project should encode this not as a simple abandonment of colour, but as a ranking of signs:

`skin colour`
= visible but accidental / weak for origin inference;

`language`
= stronger historical/genealogical evidence under the relevant programme.

Where linguistic information is unavailable or unreliable, bodily colour may remain a fallback descriptor rather than the preferred organising principle.

This creates a new epistemic distinction:

`perceptual discriminability`
≠
`classificatory warrant`
≠
`genealogical / causal warrant`.

## 9. A second negative jurisdiction of colour

The project now has at least two sharply distinct actor-grounded negative controls.

### Schlözer 1772

`colour at fact proposition`
→ historian colours the Factum
→ illegitimate because evidentiary content is altered.

### Gatterer / Araújo control

`literal visible colour`
→ empirically observable difference
→ proposed classifier
→ insufficient as essential / genealogical organising principle
→ demoted.

These are different failure modes:

1. `fact_alteration_failure`;
2. `classification_warrant_failure`.

The second is especially important because nothing about the visible colour itself need be false. The problem is inferential jurisdiction.

## 10. Refined research question

The colour project can now ask two nested questions:

> **What may colour do to history?**

and

> **When is a visible difference allowed to become a historical difference?**

The second formulation extends the project beyond pigment while remaining colour-specific: it asks when colour can move from perceptual property to classificatory, causal, genealogical or organisational function.

## 11. Historiographical boundary

Araújo already establishes prior art for:

- the substantive epistemological content of the Gatterer–Schlözer Federkrieg;
- the contrast between Gatterer's empirical orientation and Schlözer's methodological Plan;
- the problem of representing historical parts in Zusammenhang;
- chronological and synchronistic representation as core Göttingen methodological problems;
- Gatterer's treatment and later subordination of accidental bodily differences including skin colour.

Therefore novelty cannot be claimed for these points alone.

The bounded opening remains the cross-domain governance of colour operations:

- colour as visible object-property;
- colour as candidate classifier;
- colour as rejected or demoted organising principle;
- colour as graphical relational syntax;
- colour as decoder;
- colour as copy-level pigment;
- colour as rhetorically suspect intervention in facts.

## 13. Current synthesis

> **In Göttingen universal history, making relations visible was itself a methodological task. But visibility did not determine warrant. A visible bodily difference could fail as an organising principle, just as a visually forceful table could exceed the warrant of the classifications it displayed. Colour is therefore most productive as a test of the boundary between perception, classification, relation and historical justification.**
