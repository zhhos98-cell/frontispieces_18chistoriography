# HLQ Figure 8 delta — Gatterer, Köhler, and heraldic hatching, 1774

Date: 2026-09-04
Status: high-value source delta; article core unchanged

## Result

A much stronger first-order candidate for the Gatterer return exists in Gatterer's *Abriß der Heraldik* (2nd ed., Nürnberg: Raspe, 1774), p. 12 and Tab. II figs. 19–20.

The passage does three things in one place:

1. discusses Rink's additional hatching signs for `Naturfarbe` and `Eisenfarbe`;
2. evaluates the signs against Gatterer's own reading of old `Wappenbriefe`;
3. explicitly directs the reader to an earlier `köhlerische Programma` for the history/origin of heraldic hatchings.

This is more discriminating for the present colour-bookhistory article than a generic claim that Gatterer inherited a Nürnberg publication environment. It is Gatterer himself, in a printed heraldic handbook, discussing graphic colour notation, source usage, and Köhler in the same paragraph.

## 1. Controlled wording

Several independent modern heraldic references reproduce the same passage and assign it to Gatterer, *Abriß der Heraldik* (Nürnberg, 1774), p. 12:

`Der sel. Rink zu Altdorf hat zu den Schrafirungen des Colombiere noch zwo, nämlich das Zeichen der natürlichen (Fig. 19.) und der Eisenfarbe (Fig. 20.) hinzusetzt: ich halte jedoch die erste für überflüssig, die andere aber um deswillen für unterweislich, weil ich aus alten Wappenbriefen gelernet habe, daß die Herolde, wenigstens in Deutschland, die Eisenfarbe und die weise farbe, das ist, Silber als gleichgültige Ausdrücke gebrauchen. Zur Erläuterung dieser Abhandlung vom Ursprunge der Schrafirungen dient das oben angeführte köhlerische Programma.`

## 4. Köhler's Programma is identifiable

The `köhlerische Programma` can be identified as Johann David Köhler's 1736 Göttingen programme:

*Programma De Inventoribus Incisurarum Gallice Les Hachures Dictarum Quibus Metalla Et Colores In Tesseris Gentilitiis Absque Pigmentis Indicantur: Praemissum Lectionibus Heraldicis Privatis Hoc Semestri Spatio Aestivo B. C. D. A Se Habendis* (Gottingae: Ioh. Frid. Hageri, 1736).

A recent University of Edinburgh thesis gives the shorter bibliographic form:

Johann David Köhler, *Programma De Inventoribus Incisurarum Gallice Les Hachures Dictarum* (Gottingae: Ioh. Frid. Hageri, 1736), n.p.

Modern heraldic bibliography also records a 1758 reprint appended to Immanuel Weber's *Examen artis heraldicae*, pp. 137–152.

This substantially sharpens the relation:

`Köhler 1736 programme specifically on hachures used to indicate metals and colours without pigments`
→ `Gatterer 1774 discussion of specific hatching signs`
→ `Gatterer explicitly directs reader back to Köhler's programme`.

This is a direct textual relation and does not depend on an inferred influence chain.

## 5. Why this matters for the article while staying first-order

The article does NOT need to say that Nürnberg heraldry caused Göttingen universal history.

The first-order book-history use is narrower:

- Nürnberg armorial books repeatedly carried colour through monochrome hatching and explanation;
- Köhler wrote a named programme specifically on the inventors/origins of hachures by which metals and colours are indicated without pigments;
- Gatterer later discusses colour hatchings in his own heraldic handbook, tests one distinction against old Wappenbriefe, and points the reader to Köhler's programme;
- the same Gatterer published the copy-variable coloured *Synopsis* and worked inside the former Weigel/Köhler publication ecology.

No abstract transfer mechanism is required.
