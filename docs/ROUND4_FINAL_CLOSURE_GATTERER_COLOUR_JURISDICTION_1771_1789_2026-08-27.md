## 1. 1771 — visible difference does not automatically become essential difference

Araújo's exact-locus reconstruction of Gatterer, *Einleitung in die synchronistische Universalhistorie* (1771), pp.61–62, reports outward human differences as `zufällige Unterscheidungszeichen` rather than `wesentliche` differences and as `blose Varietäten`.

Evidence status:

`TEXT-DERIVATIVE / SECONDARY SCHOLARLY CONTROL / EXACT PRIMARY LOCUS / NOT PAGE-IMAGE CONTROLLED`

Closure claim:

> Gatterer already restricts the inferential authority of visible bodily difference before the 1775 Anthropographia programme: perceptible variation does not by itself prove essential human difference or separate origin.

## 2. 1775 — bodily shape and colour belong to geographical Anthropographia

Vermeulen reproduces Gatterer 1775 pp.4–5 verbatim enough to control the four-part architecture:

`Gränzkunde / Länderkunde / Staatenkunde / Menschen- und Völkerkunde (Anthropographia und Ethnographia)`.

Gatterer explicitly says the terms are being used in a **geographical**, not historical, political, or statistical meaning.

Fischer's reconstruction of the *Inhaltsanzeige* further places `Geographie der Menschenkörper nach Gestalt und Farbe[n]/Hautfarbe[n]` as the first programme under the fourth part, with geography of languages treated separately.

Evidence status:

`PP.4–5 = SECONDARY EXACT QUOTATION OF PRIMARY LOCUS / NOT PAGE-IMAGE CONTROLLED`

`CONTENTS / BODY-COLOUR HEADING = SECONDARY SCHOLARLY RECONSTRUCTION, EXACT LOCUS / NOT PAGE-IMAGE CONTROLLED`

Closure claim:

> Bodily shape and colour have legitimate **descriptive-geographical** jurisdiction in Gatterer's Anthropographia. The programme does not by itself authorize essential, causal, or genealogical conclusions from colour.

A bibliographic/formal qualification is important: the fourth-part architecture is substantially programmatic in the *Abriß*, not a fully executed empirical anthropographic treatise. Round 4 therefore closes the **field architecture and permitted operation**, not a non-existent exhaustive 1775 colour taxonomy.

## 3. 1789 — origin inference is weighted toward language

Araújo's exact-locus reconstruction of Gatterer, *Kurzer Begriff der Geographie*, vol.2 (1789), p.606, reports classification of peoples by `Hauptsprachen` and treats language as the stronger evidentiary route to their origin.

The further claim that skin colour remains a fallback specifically because precise/reliable linguistic information is lacking is retained at a lower evidentiary grade: it is Araújo's interpretation of the locus rather than presently controlled actor wording.

Evidence status:

`LANGUAGE > COLOUR FOR ORIGIN = TEXT-DERIVATIVE / SECONDARY SCHOLARLY EXACT-LOCUS CONTROL / NOT PAGE-IMAGE CONTROLLED`

`COLOUR AS DATA-SCARCITY FALLBACK = SECONDARY INTERPRETATION / PROBABLE`

Closure claim:

> By 1789, colour may remain descriptively available, but language carries stronger genealogical/origin warrant.

## 4. Gatterer's task-specific jurisdiction — final result

The best bounded longitudinal model is:

`visible bodily difference`
→ can be perceived and described

`bodily colour / shape`
→ can organize programmatic geographical Anthropographia

but

`visible bodily difference`
≠ sufficient evidence of `wesentliche` difference

and

`bodily colour`
< language / `Hauptsprachen` for origin inference.

Canonical formulation:

> **Gatterer separates perceptual discriminability, descriptive classificatory utility, essential difference, and genealogical origin. A visible property can be useful in the first two jurisdictions without being sufficient in the latter two.**

This is the central Round 4 result.

## 5. Boundary against Zimmermann and Meiners

The four-round comparison can now be closed without adding another actor.

### Gatterer

His distinctive operation is **task separation**. Visible bodily difference is permitted descriptively but its authority is restricted when the target becomes essence or origin.

### Zimmermann 1778

Zimmermann builds a more expansive generative model:

- climatic intensity;
- bodily plasticity;
- colour gradation;
- inheritance and reproductive isolation;
- migration and mixture;
- climate as a classificatory principle;
- conjectural common-origin historical reconstruction.

His central issue is how differences can be **produced, graded, and historically distributed**.

### Meiners 1785–1793

Meiners operates inside much of the same causal field but makes:

- rank structure more explicit;
- anti-single-trait rules more explicit;
- specimen provenance and isolated-body-part inference more tightly regulated.

His distinctive change is stronger **rank stabilization and evidential regulation**.

### Comparative closure

> **Gatterer partitions the tasks a visible difference may legitimately perform; Zimmermann develops historical-geographical machinery for producing and classifying variation; Meiners further stabilizes ranks and regulates bodily evidence.**

This is a structural comparator, not a direct influence chain.
