# Project scope correction — Gatterer first — 26 August 2026

## Primary research object

The project is centrally about **Johann Christoph Gatterer's images and the contemporary methodological disputes over historical knowledge in which those images participated**.

The principal questions are therefore not primarily:

- how one frontispiece travelled across the entire eighteenth century;
- how the Weigel publishing house reused copperplates in general;
- or how genealogy/heraldry calendars evolved as a genre for their own sake.

Those questions remain useful only insofar as they clarify Gatterer's visual and methodological intervention.

## Correct direction of explanation

The active explanatory direction is:

`Gatterer image / visual apparatus`
→ `what historical operation the image claims or enables`
→ `how genealogy, heraldry, chronology, numismatics, documents and other witnesses are ranked or coordinated`
→ `which contemporary methodological controversy this answers or reframes`
→ `what inherited visual/material infrastructure Gatterer appropriated, retained, altered or rejected`

The earlier Weigel, Eimmart, Steidlin, Köhler, Eccard and related material is therefore **prehistory, control and production context**, not the project's governing narrative.

## Status of work already completed

The previous long-run plate and publication-history work remains valuable. It has established, among other things:

- an older Eimmart Wappenbuch threshold lineage predating Gatterer;
- the Müller–Tyroff `Historia / Genealogia / Heraldica / Testes temporum et veritatis` frontispiece within the Weigel Wappenkalender;
- copy-level variation and material recomposition across calendars, handbooks and Wappenbücher;
- Köhler/Eccard/Döderlein cases in which heraldic or numismatic images functioned as historical evidence rather than decoration;
- the 1753–62 Tyroff–Weigel–Gatterer production transition.

These findings should now be **re-routed toward Gatterer** rather than allowed to determine the article's chronology or centre of gravity.

## Immediate research priorities after correction

1. Identify and collate the specific Gatterer images at issue, including their inscriptions, signatures, dates, placement and plate states.
2. Reconstruct Gatterer's own statements on historical method, especially the relation among genealogy, heraldry, chronology, geography, diplomatics, numismatics, source criticism and universal history.
3. Reconstruct contemporary methodological disputes and competing programmes in which Gatterer's visual apparatus becomes intelligible.
4. Distinguish what Gatterer inherits materially from Weigel/Tyroff from what he changes epistemically or pedagogically.
5. Use pre-Gatterer comparators only when they test a concrete claim about Gatterer's operation.

## Working rule

**Gatterer-first, image-to-method.**

Do not let provenance accumulation become the argument. Plate genealogy is evidence when it changes the interpretation of Gatterer's image, its evidentiary role, its pedagogical function, or its position in contemporary historical method.
