### Kant lecture strata: direct textual controls, date-sensitive

**Herder lecture notes, 1763–64.** The BBAW Kant Digital transcript of the Herder octavo notes already contains a cluster later familiar from Kant's physical geography: Black infants and postnatal darkening; illness/burns producing white skin; the disputed Cape Verde Portuguese example; Black–White admixture sequences; white children born to Black parents with white woolly hair and abnormal eyes; and the explicit question of how a colour first attributed to air/sun becomes hereditary. This is an early lecture-note control showing that colour, hair, admixture and hereditary persistence were already separable variables decades before Meiners 1792.

### Buffon 1766: historical actor model of differential causal depth

Buffon's *De la dégénération des animaux* (Histoire naturelle, tome XIV, 1766) provides a stronger upstream conceptual control. In Buffon's own causal architecture:
- skin, hair and eye colour are among the more exterior/superficial characters and can change under climate relatively readily;
- stature, facial form and the quality/form of hair are treated as more deeply produced characters that require additional causes such as food/land and longer temporal exposure;
- the action of air/sky is described as more superficial, especially on skin colour, whereas nourishment acts on internal form;
- sufficiently prolonged changes may eventually be transmitted through generation and stabilize as population characters.

Methodological consequence:
- the project should distinguish **distributed phenotype** (traits can disagree) from **stratified phenotype** (traits are assigned different causal depth, response time and hereditary persistence);
- this is not a modern surface/depth metaphor imposed on the source. Buffon's own argument historically stratifies causal depth;
- this permits a precise Wilson comparison: Wilson warns against assuming a modern ontological `surface` over a truer `depth`, while Buffon gives us an actor's own historically situated surface/depth causal model that must itself be analyzed as evidence.

### Maupertuis 1744/45: earlier heredity problem-space

Maupertuis's anonymous 1744 dissertation / 1745 *Vénus physique* supplies an earlier control on anomalous colour and inheritance. White children born to Black parents and other hereditary anomalies are used to reason about biparental contribution and the possibility that an apparently accidental character can become hereditary. Kant's Herder lecture notes explicitly mention Maupertuis's *Vénus physique* in the surrounding breeding/race discussion. This creates a controlled upstream problem-space, though not yet a demonstrated direct line from Maupertuis to Meiners.

### Meiners against single-part inference

A recent critical volume quotes Meiners, *Grundriß der Geschichte der Menschheit*, p. 23, objecting that neither whole peoples nor individual persons can be judged from the formation of a single body part, and that the characteristic formation of one part across whole nations cannot be inferred from isolated bones. The exact primary page remains to be recovered, but the quotation strongly supports the current reconstruction:

`single bodily trait ≠ whole-population warrant`.

This is compatible with the 1792 trait-decoupling evidence and gives a broader methodological statement beyond colour.

### Girtanner 1796 as later formalization

Christoph Girtanner's *Über das Kantische Princip für die Naturgeschichte* (Göttingen: Vandenhoeck & Ruprecht, 1796) is available as an ETH/e-rara public-domain digital object with IIIF and OCR. Blumenbach incorporated a reference to Girtanner in the 1797 fifth edition of the *Handbuch der Naturgeschichte* when distinguishing `Rasse` from `Spielart`; the later text defines race as a character generated through degeneration that is necessarily inherited in reproduction, whereas ordinary varieties need not reproduce with such necessity.

This is useful as a downstream formalization of hereditary persistence, not evidence for what Meiners knew in 1792 unless a direct connection is independently established.

### Hard methodological closures

1. **Meiners 1792 is not well described as a simple climate → heredity replacement.** The near-contemporary Krünitz witness indicates that parental blood has highest default causal weight by 1792, while climate, local environment, mixture and internal physiology remain genuine causal channels. Code this as `causal reprioritisation within a plural causal model`.

2. **Phenotypic equifinality is explicit.** Similar visible colour can be assigned different causal histories: descent/admixture in one case, local climatic modification in another. Therefore `same visible outcome ≠ same causal history`.

3. **Hauptfarben and Urfarben are analytically distinct.** The derivative witness preserves four principal colours but only two original colours. A descriptive category can therefore be stable while being secondary at the genealogical/causal level.

4. **Trait decoupling is required.** In the admixture material, colour can change across generations without facial/body form changing at the same rate. `colour`, morphology, hair and other bodily traits cannot be assumed to move as a single phenotype.

5. **Distributed phenotype is not enough; a stratified phenotype model is historically warranted.** Buffon supplies actor-explicit causal stratification: some visible characters are treated as more rapidly responsive / superficial, others as slower / more formative, with different hereditary persistence. New core chain:

`visible trait → causal susceptibility → response time → hereditary persistence → evidentiary weight`.

6. **The inheritance-conversion problem predates Meiners.** Kant's 1763–64 Herder notes explicitly raise the problem of how a colour attributed to air/sun could become hereditary. Meiners therefore enters an existing problem-space rather than inventing it.

7. **Cross-trait weighting is part of the evidence architecture.** A genealogical inference must be reconstructed as a weighted bundle rather than `colour → descent`:

`colour × morphology × hair × geography/provenance × migration/descent report × witness credibility → causal/genealogical attribution`.

8. **Standardisation remains only one comparability technology.** None of the above removes the earlier distinction between material swatch calibration and witness/report calibration. Greater descriptive precision still does not automatically expand causal or genealogical jurisdiction.
