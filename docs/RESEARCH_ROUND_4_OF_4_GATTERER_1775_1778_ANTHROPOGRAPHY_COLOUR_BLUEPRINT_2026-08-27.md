## Consequential publication/structure control

Modern scholarship reports two facts requiring primary verification:

1. the book is dated 1775, while Gatterer's `Vorerinnerung` reportedly states that publication was delayed until 1778;
2. the work is incomplete: only the first two major geographical divisions were written out, while `Staatenkunde` and `Menschen- und Völkerkunde` survive as programme/outline material in the introductory `Inhaltsanzeige`.

If primary front matter confirms this, the project must not describe the bodily-colour item as an executed chapter or a completed classification system.

The correct evidence category would instead be:

`projected disciplinary architecture / programme-level classification`.
