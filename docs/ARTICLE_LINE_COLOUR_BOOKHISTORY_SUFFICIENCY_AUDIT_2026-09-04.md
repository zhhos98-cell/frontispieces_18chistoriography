## NODE II — Nürnberg retrospective: MORE THAN SUFFICIENT

This is the evidentiary centre of the article.

### A. Monochrome colour decoding

The Wappenkalender corpus repeatedly contains a full-page engraved `Erklärung der Farben` across the 1728–1757 range in physical-copy descriptions.

The 1744 direct witness gives the strongest closed book-historical sequence:

`engraved colour key`
+ `armorial plate using matching hatching`
+ `later prose blazon naming the tinctures`.

This supports a first-order claim that chromatic information can be materially absent as pigment while remaining recoverable through graphic and verbal channels.

A 1729 incomplete copy lacking the colour-key leaf further proves that corpus presence and decoder completeness can diverge at copy level.

### B. Earlier colouring workflow

Siebmacher / Spener controls provide a compact prehistory:

- verbal tincture declaration can guide later colouring;
- letter coding can carry colour in monochrome printing;
- dot/line hatching offers another graphic carrier;
- prose remains necessary where graphic scale becomes insufficient.

This material is sufficient as a short prehistory and should not expand into a separate seventeenth-century article.

### C. Serial updating and book production

The 1728 and 1733 Wappenkalender direct witnesses show:

- annual revision and augmentation;
- `neuesten Nachrichten` and correspondence feeding new data;
- tables designed for `täglichen Zeitungs-Lesen`;
- terminal `Zusätze / Verbesserungen / Veränderungen` added while printing is already underway.

This is strong first-order evidence for how colour/heraldic material belonged to a changing serial reference book rather than a static armorial plate tradition.

### D. 1772 publisher retrospective

Primary-direct and especially valuable.

The publisher retrospectively identifies:

- excessively small figures;
- letter-coded tinctures producing `Unordnung und Mißverstand`;
- insufficient space for descriptions;
- arms changing `von Zeit zu Zeit`;
- Köhler 1723 as an attempt to remedy earlier format problems.

This is sufficient without requiring a modern theoretical vocabulary.

### E. Copy / matrix resolution

Physical continuity of one colour-key copper across all annuals is unresolved, but **not required** for this article unless a same-matrix claim is made.

The article can safely write recurrent colour-key module / recurring decoder architecture without claiming one physical matrix.

### Sufficiency judgment

The Nürnberg section is already overdetermined by primary and copy-level material. No broad search is justified.

## HINGE — Nürnberg to Gatterer: SUFFICIENT, colour-specific continuity PARTLY OPEN

### Secure bridges

1. 1755: Gatterer works in Nürnberg and the Tyroff/Weigel visual-production environment.
2. 1756: Gatterer contributes six numismatic pieces plus a Köhler biographical preface to the posthumous closure of Köhler's `Münz-Belustigung`.
3. 1759: Gatterer's `Handbuch der neuesten Genealogie und Heraldik` remains in the inherited publication ecology and carries familiar paired armorial/genealogical architecture.
4. 1759–1766: the same publication sequence is repeatedly refreshed, expanded, re-annualized, and given an embedded/attached `Abriß der Heraldik` with eight explanatory copperplates.
5. The older Wappenkalender colour-key / hatching / prose-description system is explicitly recognized in the repository as the local decoder layer against which Gatterer's later general heraldic teaching apparatus can be compared.

### One important open book-historical test

It is **not yet established at direct copy level that Gatterer's 1759 Handbuch retained the same `Erklärung der Farben` legend/copper used in the pre-1758 Wappenkalender**.

This is the most valuable remaining bridge check because it is perfectly aligned with the article's first-order colour/book-history core.

But it is not fatal. The bridge does not depend on proving colour-key matrix continuity: editorial/publication succession and the transition from armorial reference apparatus to Gatterer teaching apparatus are already secure.

### Sufficiency judgment

Enough to draft. One bounded 1759 colour-key check would materially strengthen the hinge.
