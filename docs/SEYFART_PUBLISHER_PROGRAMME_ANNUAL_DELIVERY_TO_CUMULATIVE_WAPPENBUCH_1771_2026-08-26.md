# Seyfart/Raspe 1771 publisher programme: annual delivery into a cumulative Wappenbuch — 26 August 2026

## Result

The 1771 *Vorbericht* to *Der durchlauchtigen Welt vollständiges Wappenbuch*, vol. 1, supplies contemporary actor-level evidence for the material relation between the older *Wappencalender* and the cumulative *Wappenbuch*. It substantially upgrades the retrospective reconstruction given by Christian Samuel Theodor Bernd in 1830.

The publisher does not present the *Wappenbuch* as an unrelated new compilation. The programme explicitly says that the arms previously used in the *Wappencalender* were taken as the basis, that the expanded work was planned in four large-octavo volumes, and that because the necessary armorial material could only be assembled gradually, the material would be delivered in separate instalments and issued annually together with the *Wappencalender*.

The relation is therefore explicit at publication-programme level:

`existing Wappencalender armorial stock`
→ `basis for expanded Wappenbuch`
→ `material gathered progressively`
→ `delivery in separate Abtheilungen`
→ `annual issue together with Wappencalender`
→ `eventual multi-volume cumulative Wappenbuch`.

## Contemporary statements

### 1. The old Wappencalender is named as the predecessor

The *Vorbericht* calls the earlier object the known `genealogisch-heraldische Handbuch oder Wappencalender` and says it had continued annually with substantial approval, especially improved and enlarged in the later years through Gatterer's work.

This establishes self-conscious continuity at the level of genre/object identity rather than requiring a later bibliographer to infer the succession.

### 2. The expanded Wappenbuch responds to demand for a more complete visual order

The publisher says there was demand for the arms of additional German ecclesiastical/secular princes and counts and of foreign princes and counts to appear in suitable size and order and with correct descriptions, so that a `möglichst vollständiges Wappenbuch der jetzt lebenden hohen Häuser` might result.

The organising ambition is therefore simultaneously:

- greater coverage;
- controlled image size/order;
- correct verbal descriptions;
- present-oriented completeness for living high houses.

The *Wappenbuch* is not defined merely by rebinding. It is an explicit programme of visual and descriptive reordering.

### 3. Existing Wappencalender plates/images are the declared basis

The decisive sentence is:

`Man hat die bisher bey dem Wappencalender gewesenen Wappen zum Grunde gelegt`.

This is direct contemporary evidence that the cumulative project begins by treating the existing calendar armorial corpus as reusable substrate. It strongly supports the repository's distinction between host-publication identity and visual/plate-stock identity.

The sentence alone does not establish that every image was printed from the same physical copper matrix. `gewesenen Wappen` can securely establish content/design inheritance; matrix continuity still requires plate-level comparison.

### 4. Four-volume plan

The publisher says the work will be brought to completion in `vier großoctav Bänden`, with a planned categorical distribution: the first volume for imperial, royal, electoral and German princely arms; the second and third for imperial counts; and the fourth for princes/counts outside Germany plus intervening changes and newly arising German princely/comital arms.

This shows that the cumulative volume structure was prospective, not merely imposed later by binders or bibliographers.

### 5. Annual instalment delivery is explicit

The crucial logistical statement is:

`Da es unmöglich ist, alle diese Wappen anders als nach und nach, mit vieler Mühe und durch mannichfaltigen Beystand zusammen zu bringen; so sollen selbige in verschiedenen Abtheilungen geliefert, und jährlich mit dem Wappencalender ausgegeben werden.`

This gives a direct contemporary mechanism for de-/re-serialisation:

`cumulative endpoint`
+
`incremental information gathering`
→ `separate instalments`
→ `annual co-issue with serial Wappencalender`.

The annual calendar/handbook and cumulative Wappenbuch are thus not simply two bibliographic descriptions of the same leaves after the fact. The publisher intentionally uses the annual serial as the delivery infrastructure for a separately completable cumulative object.

### 6. Completeness is projected across years

The *Vorbericht* concludes that by following the same descriptive arrangement for the comital arms, the publisher expects `in etlichen Jahren`, together with genealogy, to deliver as complete a work as contemporary knowledge of heraldry requires.

Completeness is therefore temporally distributed. It is an endpoint generated by repeated annual delivery rather than a property of any single annual issue.

## Relation to Bernd 1830

Bernd's later account remains valuable because it supplies an additional commercial explanation: subscribers did not want to purchase the same armorial illustrations every year, so different arms came to be supplied in successive years; the resulting 571 arms and texts were independently numbered and given special titles so that they could form a general *Wappenbuch*.

The 1771 *Vorbericht* now independently controls the other half of that mechanism from within the publishing programme itself:

- prior Wappencalender armorial material is explicitly the basis;
- expansion is intentionally cumulative;
- the multi-volume architecture is prospective;
- progressive gathering is necessary;
- separate instalments are to be issued annually with the Wappencalender.

Safe synthesis:

> By 1771 the publisher explicitly described the *Wappenbuch* as a cumulative expansion built on earlier Wappencalender armorial material and distributed in instalments through the annual Wappencalender. Bernd's 1830 account adds the later retrospective explanation that subscriber resistance to repeated arms drove successive annual packets toward non-repeating content and independent numbering.

Do not collapse the two sources: the subscriber-motivation claim remains retrospective unless a closer contemporary advertisement or preface is found.

## Consequence for the frontispiece problem

This contemporary programme makes the repeated-frontispiece evidence more analytically significant. Interior armorial content is explicitly designed to accumulate in differentiated instalments, while complete-set catalogues report repeated frontispieces across the resulting volumes.

The emerging structural contrast is therefore:

`threshold image: potentially repeatable`
vs.
`interior armorial packet: progressively cumulative/differentiated`.

That is exactly the configuration in which a stable or recurring frontispiece can function as a serial/cumulative identity marker while the internal image stock changes. Image-level identification of the 1767/1768 frontispieces remains separate (manual queue M006/M007).

## Evidentiary guardrails

1. The 1771 *Vorbericht* is contemporary publisher-programme evidence, but it describes an intended and ongoing project; surviving copies remain necessary to establish how completely the plan was realised.
2. `Wappen ... zum Grunde gelegt` establishes inheritance/reuse at content/design level, not automatic same-matrix copperplate continuity.
3. The four-volume plan and annual instalment mechanism are explicit; exact year-by-year allocation must still be reconstructed from surviving annual and cumulative witnesses.
4. The claim about subscribers refusing repeated arms remains Bernd 1830 unless a contemporary source states it.
5. Frontispiece repetition is independently reported by complete-set catalogues, but composition and matrix identity require direct image comparison.
