# Herder–Schlözer 1772–1773: table, measure, strict history, and the pre-colour controversy surface

Date: 2026-08-27
Status: active controlled controversy map

## 1. Revised boundary

The colour-method branch should preserve two distinctions at once.

First, the Herder–Schlözer controversy and the Gatterer–Schlözer `Species Facti` quarrel are **different textual controversy surfaces**.

Second, they are not socially or institutionally independent. Older biographical scholarship and later Göttingen historiography place the 1772 Herder attack inside an already tense academic field around Schlözer, Heyne, Gatterer and competing historical programmes. The possibility of Heyne's encouragement of Herder's attack has long been discussed. This contextual entanglement does not license us to merge the arguments of the two polemics.

Thus the current rule is:

`distinct textual disputes`
+
`entangled Göttingen institutional field`
≠
`one colour controversy`.

### Herder–Schlözer, 1772–1773

Directly bears on:
- `Fakta`;
- `strenge Wissenschaft`;
- correctness and determinacy;
- the warrant for a universal historical `Plan`;
- commensuration across heterogeneous times and peoples;
- `Tabelle`, synopsis and system;
- graphical/pictorial delegation;
- pedagogy and memory.

### Gatterer–Schlözer, 1773

Directly bears on:
- teaching competition;
- priority and alleged plagiarism;
- ownership of a `Plan` / Universalgeschichte method;
- institutional and professional stakes.

No direct evidence yet shows that colour, illumination or a specific graphical medium was itself an explicit object of the `Species Facti` exchange.

## 2. Herder's 1772 attack is not merely anti-table

Herder's review appeared in the *Frankfurter gelehrte Anzeigen*, no. 60 (28 July 1772), pp. 473–478. The entire 1772 volume is openly digitised by Heidelberg University Library (DOI `10.11588/diglit.27329`). Modern critical scholarship reproduces and locates several passages from the review.

Herder demands more `Fakta` and history in place of declamatory chapters and asks how rhetorical `Schaumblasen` can function as a `Grundriß zur strengen Wissenschaft, der Historie`. He praises the `Richtigkeit` and `Bestimmtheit` of dry German historical textbooks while accusing Schlözer of reproducing Voltaire's attractive errors.

But the deeper methodological attack is on **measure and commensurability**. Herder asks, in effect:

- where is the single end point of human development?
- what is meant by progress of the human race?
- where is the `Maaß`?
- where are the `Data zum Maaße` for such different times and peoples?

This is a more exact diagnosis than `Herder rejects tables`.

The problem is:

`heterogeneous historical particulars`
→ selection of a common metric / developmental axis
→ comparison and commensuration
→ unified Plan / system
→ tabular or synoptic disposition.

Herder contests the warrant of the earlier operations before the material table is even reached.

## 3. The table is therefore an operator, not the first abstraction

The colour project now needs a sequence of operators rather than a binary opposition between prose and visuality.

A provisional chain is:

`source / report`
→ `criticism`
→ `Factum`
→ `measure / category / selection`
→ `Zusammenstellung / Plan`
→ `table / graphical disposition`
→ `colour / illumination`
→ `inspection / association / memory`.

This produces the key methodological correction:

> **Before asking what colour does inside a table, ask what measure, selection and tabulation have already done to historical difference.**

The table can flatten or expose relations before any pigment is added. Colour can then intensify, discriminate, rank or memorize the relations the table has already constructed.

Therefore:

`table_operator != colour_operator`.

## 4. Herder's `Maaß` question changes the colour problem

If colour is applied after a historical system has already partitioned periods, peoples, states or relations, then chromatic clarity can make a questionable classification look self-evident.

The relevant possible failure is not merely inaccurate pigment.

It is:

`weakly warranted commensuration`
→ `clean table`
→ `clear colour coding`
→ `high perceptual certainty`
without a corresponding increase in evidentiary warrant.

This gives the project a precise distinction between:

- **epistemic warrant** — why the categories/relations are historically justified;
- **perceptual evidence / Evidenz** — how immediately the relation becomes visible;
- **mnemonic force** — how strongly it is retained.

These can converge, but they are not identical.

## 5. Schlözer's 1773 reply: the graphical operator is positively defended

Schlözer reprinted Herder's review and answered it in *Vorstellung seiner Universal-Historie*, part II (Göttingen/Gotha: Dieterich, 1773), pp. [225]–416. ULB Sachsen-Anhalt identifies the institutional carrier as DOI `10.25673/95815`, VD18 `90090861`, shelfmark `AB 22 A 9/k, 26 (2)`.

A modern history-of-images study quotes p. 327. Schlözer refuses simply to ridicule `gemahlte chronologische Tabellen`, at least for children's instruction. Solomon's temple, the Egyptian pyramids and Homer may be represented together on one painted sheet. Their pictorial co-presence makes the objects vivid, while their simultaneity (`Synchronismus`) is fixed through `Association der Ideen`.

Operationally:

`historical particulars already accepted`
→ `pictorial co-presence`
→ `synchronism perceptually available`
→ `association / durable memory`.

The operation acts principally on a **relation among facts**, not on the factual warrant of the individual objects.

The exact p. 327 wording remains strong secondary quotation control until the institutional page image is directly inspected.

## 6. Schlözer does not accept an opposition between correctness and presentation

Secondary reconstructions of Schlözer's reply also preserve a revealing self-positioning: he refuses the choice between learned correctness and attractive historical presentation and wants history to be both `Mascouisch=richtig` and `Voltairisch=schön`.

This is important because it blocks another false binary:

`Herder = image / living history`
versus
`Schlözer = dry table / fact`.

Schlözer's project itself tries to combine factual discipline with presentational force. The dispute concerns the **conditions under which composition and representation remain accountable**, not whether representation should exist.

## 7. The separate 1775 illumination note introduces literal colour

A modern scholarly transcription attributes to Schlözer's revised 1775 *Vorstellung der Universal-Historie* the proposal that the five principal periods of world history be distributed across five temporal compartments, and that the unequal `Länge und Dauer` of each period be made visible to the young eye `durch Illumination der Tabelle`.

The operation is distinct from the 1773 painted-synchronism example:

`periodisation`
→ `temporal scale / compartments`
→ `illumination`
→ `relative duration becomes visually inspectable`.

The 1775 institutional edition is secure (ULB Sachsen-Anhalt DOI `10.25673/95805`, VD18 `11056576`), but the exact primary page has not yet been located. Keep the wording at `secondary_scholarship / probable` until scan control.

## 8. Relation to Schlözer's 1772 `Facta färben`

The first part of the *Vorstellung* supplies the negative limit. Schlözer assigns `Kritik` the recovery/testing of facts from annals and monuments and mocks `Voltaires` for making facts or at least colouring them. `Zusammenstellung`, by contrast, belongs to the historian.

The same actor therefore yields a layered jurisdiction:

### prohibited / suspect
`Factum`
→ historian colours or manufactures factual content.

### legitimate in principle
`critically constrained particulars`
→ selection / arrangement.

### positively defended
`historical relation`
→ graphical co-presence
→ synchronism / association.

### provisionally licensed
`historical duration`
→ tabular illumination
→ visible temporal proportion.

This is why the correct problem is not `colour versus objectivity` but **jurisdiction and authorization of historical operations**.

## 9. Historiographical guard: the table/image controversy is prior art

The project must not claim novelty for discovering that Herder criticized Schlözer's tabular/synoptic historiography or that Schlözer defended historical images and chronological tables.

Existing scholarship already discusses:
- Herder's attack on tables, synopsis, progress and universal historical measurement;
- Schlözer's mosaic/tableau metaphors and aggregate-to-system programme;
- the 1773 painted chronological-table passage;
- the 1775 `Illumination der Tabelle` proposal;
- the contrast between tabular synchrony and Herder's desire for a more continuous historical `Bild`;
- the pedagogical use of historical images.

Therefore the bounded opening must sit one analytical level lower:

> **the sequence and governance of distinct historical operators — criticism, commensuration, classification, tabulation, chromatic differentiation and mnemonic display — and the changing warrants required when information passes from one operator to the next.**

Colour is valuable because it can make the output of earlier operations perceptually powerful without itself supplying the warrant for those operations.

## 10. Two controversies: revised model

The earlier `two-contest model` should be retained but reformulated.

### Textual surface A — adequacy / representation
Herder ↔ Schlözer

Primary questions:
- what counts as strict historical science?
- what warrants a universal measure or progressive Plan?
- how much heterogeneity survives synopsis?
- what may a table/picture delegate to the eye and memory?

### Textual surface B — ownership / profession
Gatterer ↔ Schlözer

Primary questions:
- who owns or first formulated the historical `Plan`?
- what counts as borrowing/plagiarism?
- who teaches Universalgeschichte and with what professional authority?

### Shared field
The disputes occur inside an entangled Göttingen institutional ecology. The same terms — `Plan`, universal history, synchronism, teaching, system, historical method — circulate across the field.

The next task is not to prove that the arguments are identical. It is to determine whether a representational operation could become **priority-bearing methodological property**.

## 12. Current strongest formulation

The immediate methodological proposition is:

> **Colour did not enter an epistemically neutral historical field. It entered after criticism, measurement, categorisation and tabulation had already transformed historical particulars. Its methodological significance therefore lies partly in how it amplified, stabilized or exposed the outputs of those prior operations.**

Short form:

> **The table has already done something to history before colour arrives.**

This is the necessary bridge from the Herder–Schlözer controversy to the broader colour-method project.
