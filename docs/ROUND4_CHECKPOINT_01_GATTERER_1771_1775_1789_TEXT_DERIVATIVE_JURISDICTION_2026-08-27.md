## 1. Gatterer 1775 pp.4–5 — four-part geography

Han F. Vermeulen (2019) reproduces the primary passage from Gatterer's *Abriß der Geographie* (1775), pp.4–5:

> `Die ganze Erdbeschreibung ... läßt sich ... bequem unter 4 Haupttheile oder Wissenschaften bringen: 1) Gränzkunde (Horismographia), 2) Länderkunde (Chorographia), 3) Staatenkunde (Poleographia oder geographica Politice), und 4) Menschen- und Völkerkunde (Anthropographia und Ethnographia).`

The continuation states that these four terms are to be taken in **geographical** meaning, not historical, political or statistical meaning.

Source:

Han F. Vermeulen, “Before Boas: The Genesis of Ethnography and Ethnology in the German Enlightenment,” *Mitteilungen der Berliner Gesellschaft für Anthropologie, Ethnologie und Urgeschichte* 40 (2019), 91–117, quoting Gatterer 1775: 4–5.

Public PDF:

https://www.easaonline.org/downloads/networks/hoan/HOAN_Newsletter_16n_Article_Vermeulen_2019.pdf

Evidence status:

`SECONDARY EXACT QUOTATION OF PRIMARY LOCUS — HIGH CONFIDENCE — NOT PAGE-IMAGE CONTROLLED`

### Consequence

`Anthropographia und Ethnographia` are not loose synonyms for everything concerning humankind. Gatterer embeds them as the fourth **geographical** branch alongside boundary-, country-, and state-geography.

This already limits the jurisdiction of the 1775 bodily-colour programme: the primary task is geographical description/ordering, not automatically causal anthropology or proof of descent.

## 2. 1775 contents architecture — what Anthropographia is supposed to contain

Modern reconstruction by Jakob Fischer, based on Gatterer's 1775 *Inhaltsanzeige* (especially p.xxxiv), gives the fourth part a seven-chapter architecture whose first chapter is a **geography of human bodies by shape and colour**, followed separately by the geography of languages, religions, products, culture, trade, and the history/geography of geography.

The same reconstruction notes an important bibliographic/formal fact: the *Abriß* was not executed as a complete four-part empirical treatise. The later parts are substantially programmatic in the introductory/content architecture.

Source:

Jakob Fischer, *Geography and Enlightenment in the German states, c.1690–c.1815* (PhD thesis, 2014), discussion of Gatterer 1775 [1778], pp.4–5 and contents pp.xviii–xxxvi.

Evidence status:

`SECONDARY SCHOLARLY RECONSTRUCTION, EXACT PRIMARY LOCI — HIGH CONFIDENCE — NOT PAGE-IMAGE CONTROLLED`

### Consequence: colour operation in 1775

The strongest licensed reading is **programmatic descriptive anthropography**:

`human body → Gestalt / colour → geographical description and differentiation`

The architecture itself does not license:

`skin/body colour → essential human division`

or

`skin/body colour → genealogical origin`.

It is especially important that bodily form/colour and language occupy adjacent but distinct analytical chapters. The architecture permits comparison between them without making colour and language equivalent evidentiary channels.

### Wording debt: `Farben` versus `Hautfarben`

Secondary witnesses are not fully uniform:

- Araújo/repo reconstruction has `Geographie der Menschenkörper nach Gestalt und Hautfarben`;
- older and modern secondary witnesses also render the phrase as `Gestalt und Farben` / “shape and colour.”

Until the primary contents leaf is visually checked, the exact noun should be recorded as:

`Gestalt und Farbe[n]/Hautfarbe[n] — exact wording pending page-image verification`.

The conceptual operation does not depend on silently normalizing this lexical variant.

## 3. Gatterer 1771 pp.61–62 — visible bodily difference is not essential difference

André de Melo Araújo's 2012 monograph gives the exact primary locus and reproduces/reconstructs Gatterer's distinction in *Einleitung in die synchronistische Universalhistorie* (1771), pp.61–62:

- outward human differences are not `wesentliche` but `zufällige Unterscheidungszeichen`;
- in natural-historical language they are `blose Varietäten`;
- those outward differences therefore do not by themselves establish multiple essential human origins.

## 4. Gatterer 1789 vol.2 p.606 — language outranks colour for origin inference

Araújo (2012, pp.143–144) identifies Gatterer, *Kurzer Begriff der Geographie*, vol.2 (1789), p.606 as the relevant locus. His reconstruction states that peoples are divided decisively by language because language has stronger evidentiary force for their `Ursprung`; African peoples are organized `in Rücksicht auf die Hauptsprachen, welche sie reden`.

Araújo further interprets continued division by skin colour in some cases as a fallback where `genauen und zuverlässigen Nachrichten` about languages are lacking.

## 5. Final longitudinal Gatterer colour-jurisdiction model

The three loci support a bounded task-specific trajectory:

`1771 — visible bodily signs`
→ descriptively real but `zufällige` / `blose Varietäten`
→ insufficient for essential difference or separate-origin inference

`1775 — Anthropographia`
→ bodily `Gestalt + colour` programmatically legitimate for geographical description/differentiation
→ language placed in a separate adjacent geographical chapter

`1789 — Ursprung der Völker`
→ language / Hauptsprachen carries stronger genealogical-origin warrant
→ colour may remain descriptively available, with the fallback-under-data-scarcity reading presently secondary-mediated.

Canonical compact rule:

> **For Gatterer, perceptual visibility, descriptive classificatory utility, essential difference, and genealogical origin are different inferential jurisdictions. Bodily colour can be useful in the first two without being sufficient for the latter two.**

## 6. Boundary against Zimmermann and Meiners

The closed comparators now make the difference sharper.

### Gatterer 1771–1789

- restricts the entitlement of visible bodily difference at the level of essential/origin inference;
- still permits bodily form/colour as a descriptive geographical programme;
- gives language stronger warrant for peoples' origin;
- does not, in the controlled Round 4 loci, present a generative climate/migration mechanism for producing races.

### Zimmermann 1778

- measures climatic intensity;
- makes bodily colour gradable and temporally transformable;
- permits race formation through climate, inheritance, isolation/endogamy, migration and mixture;
- gives climate explicit classificatory jurisdiction in the division into races;
- keeps historical causal reconstruction modal rather than mathematically certain.

### Meiners 1785/1790/1793

- stabilizes a more explicit rank architecture;
- rejects colour as sole/principal sign while retaining it inside a wider bodily classificatory field;
- makes anti-single-body-part and provenance restrictions explicit;
- more sharply regulates what bodily evidence may stabilize group distinctions.

### Comparative boundary

> **Gatterer's distinctive control is task separation: a visible property may describe without proving essence or origin. Zimmermann expands the generative causal machinery by which visible human difference can be historically produced and classified. Meiners later adds stronger rank stabilization and explicit evidential regulation.**

This is a structural comparison, not evidence of a direct textual genealogy among the three authors.

## 7. Cross-domain bridge: bodily colour ↔ heraldry ↔ synchronistic tables

The repository contains strong evidence that Gatterer worked with several colour regimes:

- heraldic tinctures / hatching as conventional semantic coding;

## 8. What this returns to the Clio/frontispiece core

Round 4's useful export back to the original project is methodological, not anthropological detail:

`visible sign`
→ `descriptive / display operation`
→ `licensed inference`

must be kept separate.

For the Clio plate this means that making `Historia / Genealogia / Heraldica / Testes temporum et veritatis` visually co-present does not, by itself, establish the evidential warrant of every relation represented. The Gatterer colour case therefore works as a comparator for **display-to-inference jurisdiction**, not as evidence that anthropographic colour methods were transferred into the frontispiece.
