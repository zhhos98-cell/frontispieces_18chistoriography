## New falsification result — Meiners 1790 does not support absolute hereditary closure

The last write-back materially revises the provisional `persistence/descent → inference amplifier` model.

### Exact-page control

A page-specific modern transcription of Meiners, `Von den Varietäten und Abarten der Neger`, *Göttingisches Historisches Magazin* 6 (1790), p.639, reproduces a passage in which he explicitly considers both `Klima` and `Zucht` as causes that may improve the descendants of transplanted Africans. He then states that transplantation into other regions and an altered way of life can improve body and mind **without mixture with supposedly better blood**.

### Same-year corroborating structure

A substantial German transcription of `Ueber die Natur der Afrikanischen Neger` preserves three compatible operations:

1. environmental transfer can produce `Ausartung` even in a population described as possessing inherited (`angestammt`) qualities;
2. mixture is treated as transmitting colour, bodily formation and mental/moral properties;
3. external and internal gifts, like natural defects, are said to `fortpflanzen` in certain lineages (`Geschlechtern`).

This is not a single-cause hereditarian architecture.

## Hard methodological revision

Replace the too-simple contrast:

`Meiners = hereditary persistence as inferential amplifier`

with:

`Meiners = hereditary/descent weighting inside a ranked plural causal field`.

More precisely:

`descent / inherited disposition` may receive high default causal weight,

while

`climate + transplantation + Zucht / life-way + mixture + education/contact`

remain active causal channels.

Therefore:

`highest default causal weight ≠ causal exclusivity`;

and

`hereditary ≠ immutable`.

This is the strongest upstream control yet for the 1792 colour reconstruction.

## Revised Blumenbach–Meiners contrast

### Blumenbach 1797

`visible difference → necessary inheritance test → Rasse / Spielart category gate`.

Persistence determines categorical jurisdiction.

### Meiners 1790–1792

`descent / blood + climate + transplantation + life-way + mixture + physiology + witness evidence → weighted causal attribution → historical / evaluative inference`.

Persistence/descent functions as a causal prior or weight rather than an exclusive binary gate.

This difference is more precise than saying one thinker is simply more or less 'hereditarian'.

## Political jurisdiction remains distinct

The 1790 political argument still expands anthropological claims into unequal-rights reasoning. The causal chain should be reconstructed as:

`travel-report / bodily / behavioural evidence`
`→ alleged natural or inherited population differences`
`→ capacity / disposition ranking`
`→ rights and duties made proportional to alleged capacities`
`→ unequal political status / defence of slavery`.

The p.639 reversibility passage does not cancel that political hierarchy. It shows that the hierarchy could coexist with a doctrine of partial intergenerational improvement.
