`Fragmente derjenigen Regeln und sich darauf gründende Noten, welche von denen zur Revision der Winckelmannschen Chronik verordnet gewesenen Fürstlich Casselschen und Darmstädtischen Commissarien zur Beobachtung vorgeschrieben und gemacht worden.`

- shelfmark: `Ms. 309`;
- Kalliope item: `eadDE-611-HS-3888458`;
- holding institution: Universitätsbibliothek Marburg;
- date: 1687;
- extent: 180 leaves.

This is enough to establish the existence of a material **rule → note → revision** apparatus. The remaining high-value questions require the manuscript itself: rule headings, commissioner names/hands, note-to-rule mapping and alignment to specific Chronicle revisions.

Repeated public searches and Kalliope's current public metadata expose no page images or full transcription. The item is therefore an archive/direct-reproduction task, not an unresolved discovery problem.

## 1. What is closed

The following can be stated without qualification beyond normal catalogue dependence:

- a 1687 manuscript witness survives at Marburg;
- it comprises 180 leaves;
- its title explicitly describes **rules** and **notes grounded on those rules**;
- those rules/notes were prescribed/made by commissioners appointed to revise the Winckelmann Chronicle;
- the catalogue itself distinguishes Cassel and Darmstadt commission authority.

This materially strengthens the reconstruction of revision as an operational apparatus rather than an abstract act of censorship.

Working relation:

`commission appointment`
→ `prescribed rules`
→ `notes grounded on rules`
→ `revision work`.

## 2. What remains manuscript-only

- named commissioners;

No roster-based reconstruction should be promoted as commissioner identity without a direct documentary link.

## 3. Six-member commission guardrail remains in force

Thomas Fuchs's public/scholarly reconstruction controls a 1686 six-person commission composed of three Marburg and three Gießen professors. The `Ms. 309` catalogue title instead speaks institutionally of princely Cassel/Darmstadt commissioners.

These descriptions may refer to overlapping stages/personnel, but they must not be silently collapsed.

Current safe distinction:

`1686 SIX-PROFESSOR COMMISSION STRUCTURE = SCHOLARLY CONTROLLED`

`1687 MS.309 RULE/NOTE COMMISSION APPARATUS = ITEM-LEVEL CATALOGUE CONTROLLED`

`PERSONNEL EQUALITY = UNPROVED`.
