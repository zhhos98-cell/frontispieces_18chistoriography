# M028 — Meiners 1792 primary verification matrix and derivative-locator correction

Date: 2026-08-27

## Purpose

This note converts M028 from a broad `read pp.611–672` task into a finite falsification matrix. The goal of the next primary pass is **not** to re-theorize the article. It is to test the exact claims currently reconstructed from Krünitz and page-specific modern quotation controls against Meiners's original 1792 text.

Primary target:
- Christoph Meiners, `Ueber die Farben, und Schattierungen verschiedener Völker`, *Neues Göttingisches historisches Magazin* I/4 (1792), pp.611–672.
- Internet Archive identifier: `neuesgttingisch00meingoog`.
- BSB shelfmark: `H.misc. 201-1`, URN `urn:nbn:de:bvb:12-bsb10738733-5`.

Current governing rule:

`derivative reconstruction → primary falsification`, not `derivative reconstruction → assumed original wording`.

## A. Claims with exact or near-exact primary-page controls already available

### A1. Programmatic problem-setting — p.612

Modern page-specific quotation control places on p.612 Meiners's opening research questions:
- whether colour is a secure mark of races / descent;
- how many `Haupt-Farben` should be admitted;
- whether one or several of those principal colours are `Grund-Farben` from which others arose.

Primary test:
- verify exact wording, punctuation and whether `Racen`, `Abkunft/Abstammung`, `Haupt-Farben`, and `Grund-Farben` are all present in the original opening.

Expected consequence:
- if confirmed, the article is explicitly framed as **causal + genealogical + classificatory**, not merely descriptive colour taxonomy.

### A2. External/environmental causal menu — pp.614–615

Modern exact-page quotation control attributes to pp.614–615 a long causal inventory including:
- solar intensity / mildness;
- heat / cold;
- air and wind;
- dryness / humidity;
- latitude, altitude, openness/closedness of terrain, proximity to sea;
- weaker or doubtful roles for food/drink;
- life-way and occupation as amplifiers/moderators rather than true co-causes;
- dirt as masking rather than changing true skin colour.

Primary test:
- verify the distinction between `true cause`, `modifier/amplifier`, and `mere masking`.

Expected consequence:
- confirms that Meiners's causal model contains differentiated causal jurisdiction, not an undifferentiated list of influences.

### A3. Admixture / generational convergence — pp.640–641

Modern page-specific controls place on pp.640–641:
- a three-generation rule for descendants of Black or American mothers to become equal in appearance/status to white fathers;
- discussion of innumerable `Zwitter-Racen` and mixed colours from combinations among groups.

Primary test:
- verify whether this is presented descriptively, normatively, genealogically, or all three;
- code exactly which traits are said to converge, and whether colour and morphology are synchronized.

Expected consequence:
- tests `trait_decoupling`, `intergenerational reversibility`, and the political/social inference attached to visible descent.

### A4. Four principal colours / two original colours — near conclusion, primary page to fix exactly

A modern scholarly quotation reproduces Meiners's sentence:

`Unter diesen vier Hauptfarben halte ich die weisse und gelbe für die Urfarben ...`

and attributes the article's concluding discussion to p.672.

The quoted continuation treats:
- white and yellow as original colours;
- black as a transformation of yellow under Indian heat plus other causes;
- red as a transformation of yellow in America through still unknown physical forces;
- similarity in body formation and mental/moral dispositions as part of the rationale.

Primary test:
- identify exact start/end pages of this paragraph;
- verify whether `Urfarben` or `Grundfarben` is the operative term in the original conclusion;
- verify the causal relation among yellow, black and red;
- verify whether morphology and mental/moral dispositions are explicitly bundled into the argument.

Expected consequence:
- confirms or falsifies the repo distinction:

`phenomenal Hauptfarbe ≠ genealogical/original colour status`.

### A5. Colour as insufficiently self-interpreting race evidence — pp.611–612

Modern race-theory scholarship cites pp.611–612 when noting that Meiners recognized colour could not serve as a simple adequate determinant of race.

Primary test:
- determine whether the original explicitly limits colour's race-diagnostic sufficiency or only frames the issue as a question to be investigated.

Expected consequence:
- this is crucial for `inference_jurisdiction`: a visible colour may be evidentially relevant without being self-sufficient.

## B. Central claims currently controlled only through the Krünitz derivative

`... wenn nicht die Farbe aller Menschen am meisten von dem Blute der Eltern abhinge.`

**Important locator rule:**
- `675` is a Krünitz derivative page, **not** a Meiners 1792 page.

Expected consequence:

`highest default causal weight ≠ causal exclusivity`.

### B2. Internal physiological colour changes

Krünitz preserves examples involving:

Primary test:

Expected consequence:

### B3. Light American populations: genealogy versus local climatic lightening

Current reconstruction uses the derivative witness to distinguish:

- relatively light skin explained climatically without European admixture in others.

Primary test:
- locate both cases;
- identify what additional evidence makes Meiners choose genealogy in one case and environment in another.

Expected consequence:
- primary closure for:

`same visible outcome ≠ same causal history`.

### B4. Trait decoupling in admixture sequences

The existing method note contains the phrase `p.679 trait-decoupling case`.

This locator is **not valid as a Meiners primary page**, because the article ends on p.672.

Working correction:
- `p.679` must be treated as a **derivative-witness locator or an unresolved erroneous locator** until the original passage is found;
- do not cite `Meiners 1792, p.679`.

Primary test:
- find the original passage in pp.611–672 where colour changes across generations while facial/body formation allegedly remains more European;

Expected consequence:

`colour change rate ≠ morphology change rate`.

This is one of the highest-value falsification items in M028.

If the original confirms:
