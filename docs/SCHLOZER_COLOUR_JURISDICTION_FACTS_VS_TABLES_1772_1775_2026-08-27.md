# Schlözer: colour jurisdiction between facts and tables, 1772–1775

Date: 2026-08-27
Status: active primary-control note; bibliographic correction incorporated

## 1. Finding

The colour-method branch now has an actor-level distinction internal to Schlözer himself. The relevant opposition is not `Schlözer = anti-colour` versus `Gatterer = pro-colour`.

The stronger distinction is:

`colouring the Facta`
≠
`graphically / chromatically arranging relations among Facta`.

Three controls must be kept bibliographically distinct:

1. **1772, part I, pp. 44–45** — direct primary control: `Facta färben` is placed on the suspect side of historical practice, whereas `Zusammenstellung` belongs to the historian and constitutes method.
2. **1773, part II, p. 327** — bibliographically fixed primary locus, wording presently controlled through a close secondary quotation: Schlözer defends the possible pedagogical value of `gemahlte chronologische Tabellen`; a painted sheet can make `Synchronismus` durable through `Association der Ideen`.
3. **1775 revised edition** — secondary quotation control: a note proposes `Illumination der Tabelle` so that the unequal length and duration of five main world-historical periods become visible to the young eye. The exact primary page still needs direct inspection.

This is an important correction to the previous pass, which had collapsed the 1773 painted-synchronism passage into the 1775 edition.

## 2. Schlözer 1772: `Facta färben` belongs on the wrong side of criticism

In *Vorstellung seiner Universal-Historie* (1772), pp. 44–45, Schlözer compares historical facts to small coloured stones of a mosaic. The historian's problem is to distribute and order them so that a whole `Gemälde` appears.

The sequence is:

`Annalen / Denkmäler`
→ `Kritik`
→ `Facta`
→ `Zusammenstellung`
→ `System / Gemälde`.

`Kritik` recovers or tests the factual units. `Zusammenstellung` is the historian's operation. Schlözer's aside against the `Voltaires` places making or at least colouring the facts (`färben sie wenigstens`) at the wrong point in that chain.

Thus:

`epistemic_location = fact_proposition`
`colour_authorization = rhetorically_suspect`.

The target is not colour or imagery in general. It is intervention in evidentiary content.

## 3. Schlözer 1773: painted synchronism is explicitly defensible

The positive control is earlier than the previous pass assumed.

The relevant text belongs to *Vorstellung seiner Universal-Historie*, **part II (1773)**:

*Hrn. Johann Gottfried Herders ... Beurteilung der Schlözerischen Universalhistorie ... mit August Ludwig Schlözers Anmerkungen über die Kunst, Universalhistorien zu beurteilen*.

ULB Sachsen-Anhalt identifies this part directly as Göttingen/Gotha: Dieterich, 1773, pp. [225]–416, shelfmark `AB 22 A 9/k, 26 (2)`, DOI `10.25673/95815`, VD18 `90090861`.

A detailed modern study quotes p. 327. Schlözer refuses simply to laugh away the idea of `gemahlte chronologische Tabellen`, at least for children's instruction. He proposes placing such objects as Solomon's temple, the Egyptian pyramids and Homer together on one painted sheet. Their co-presence should make the objects vivid and, crucially, fix their simultaneity (`Synchronismus`) through `Association der Ideen`.

Operationally:

`historical units already accepted`
→ `painted co-presence`
→ `association`
→ `memorised synchronism`.

The graphic operation acts on the **relation among facts**, not on factual warrant.

Provisional encoding:

- `epistemic_location = arrangement_system; display_inspection; memory_pedagogy`;
- `colour/image operation = co-present; relate; memorise`;
- `authorization = pedagogically_assigned`.

### Evidence guard

The 1773 work, pagination and p. 327 locus are now bibliographically secure. The quoted wording itself remains secondary-quotation control in this pass because the institutional page image has not yet been directly inspected. Promote after direct page control.

## 4. Schlözer 1775: `Illumination der Tabelle` as duration display

A second, distinct positive colour control is attributed by the same modern study to Schlözer's 1775 revised edition.

The study quotes a note in which Schlözer divides world history into five main periods and proposes putting them into a table divided into corresponding temporal compartments. Their unequal `Länge und Dauer` can then be made visible to the young eye through `Illumination der Tabelle`.

This operation differs from the 1773 painted-object example:

### 1773

`objects / events`
→ pictorial co-presence
→ synchronism remembered by association.

### 1775

`periods + duration`
→ tabular spatialisation
→ illumination
→ quantitative-temporal difference made visible.

Thus the 1775 colour referent is not an object's literal hue. It is **historical duration / temporal structure**.

Provisional encoding:

- `colour_role = relational_syntax`;
- `colour_operation = mark_duration; discriminate; support_overview`;
- `epistemic_location = arrangement_system; display_inspection; memory_pedagogy`.

ULB Sachsen-Anhalt directly identifies the 1775 second revised edition: Göttingen: Dieterich, 307 pp., shelfmark `AB 22 A 9/k, 26 (1)`, DOI `10.25673/95805`, VD18 `11056576`.

However, the modern study gives the `Illumination der Tabelle` quotation without a precise primary page locator in the surfaced passage. Therefore keep this as `secondary_scholarship / probable` until the 1775 scan is searched directly.

Do **not** cite the 1773 p. 327 pagination for this 1775 table-illumination sentence.

## 5. Same actor, three jurisdictions

The corrected chronology is stronger than a simple 1772/1775 contrast because Schlözer articulates the positive side already in the 1773 work produced during the Herder controversy.

### A. Fact layer — 1772

`Factum`
→ historian colours it
→ suspect alteration.

### B. Relational/mnemonic display — 1773

`accepted historical units`
→ painted co-presence
→ synchronism becomes vivid and memorable.

### C. Temporal/tabular display — 1775

`periodisation + duration`
→ table + illumination
→ temporal proportion becomes visually inspectable.

The governing proposition is therefore:

> **Colour or pictorialization is not legitimate or illegitimate in itself. Its status depends on what epistemic object it modifies.**

Shortest project question:

> **What may colour do to history?**

Alternative:

> **Where may colour enter without colouring the fact?**

## 6. Consequence for Gatterer–Schlözer

The old binary must be rejected:

`Schlözer = textual/conceptual`
versus
`Gatterer = visual/material`.

By 1773 Schlözer explicitly accepts at least one graphical/mnemonic delegation of synchronism. The comparison must therefore ask:

- what relation is delegated to graphic or chromatic form;
- whether the operation constructs, displays or merely helps remember the relation;
- whether colour carries unique information or redundant information;
- how the assignment is warranted;
- whether it is materially realized or remains a proposal;
- how copy variation affects the operation.

Gatterer's *Synopsis* materially realizes coloured synchronistic relations. Schlözer's 1773 and 1775 texts show that he does not object in principle to graphical intervention after factual criticism. Any real difference must therefore be sought in **scope, function, material realization and warrant**, not in a pro-/anti-colour opposition.

## 7. Relation to the controversies

The 1773 p. 327 passage belongs to Schlözer's *Anmerkungen über die Kunst, Universalhistorien zu beurteilen*, his response to Herder's hostile review. This places graphical/mnemonic method directly inside a live historiographical controversy.

That is not yet the same as the separate Gatterer–Schlözer `Species Facti` quarrel. Do not merge the two controversies.

The next question is whether the professional priority dispute with Gatterer also contains claims about `Tafel`, `Gemälde`, `Übersicht`, `Zusammenstellung`, `Plan`, `Methode`, `Auge`, `sichtbar` or related delegation vocabulary.
