Bounded test of what Eberhard August Wilhelm von Zimmermann's 1778 *Geographische Geschichte des Menschen, und der allgemein verbreiteten vierfüssigen Thiere* already permits colour, climate, geography, migration and human variation to do before Meiners 1785/1793.

## Bounded Round 3 questions

1. **Common-origin / diffusion boundary** — Does Zimmermann 1778 derive geographically dispersed human differences from one origin plus migration, climate and adaptation/transformation?
2. **Colour jurisdiction** — Is bodily colour a descriptive mark, a classificatory variable, a causal outcome, or an essential/group-defining property?
3. **Taxonomic vocabulary** — How does Zimmermann use `Race`, `Varietät`, `Stamm`, `Art`, `Geschlecht` and adjacent terms, and how stable are their relations?
4. **Intensity / degree** — Does primary actor language use `Grad`, `Maß`, `mehr/weniger`, shade/degree or analogous intensive operators in the relevant causal/classificatory argument?
5. **Boundary against Meiners** — Which operations visible in Meiners 1785/1793 are already present in Zimmermann 1778, and where does Meiners increase inferential entitlement rather than merely inherit a climate/geography model?
