# Kiefer 1755 ordinal transcription anomaly — 26 August 2026

## Problem

A current Kiefer catalogue entry for the *Geschichts-, Geschlechts- und Wappen-Calender* labels the 1755 host:

`zum vier- und dreysigstenmal ... auf das Jahr ... 1755`

and the immediately following 1756 entry also uses:

`zum vier- und dreysigstenmal ... auf das Jahr ... 1756`.

These two consecutive annuals cannot both be the thirty-fourth issue in the same continuous numbered series.

## Controls

- Deutsche Nationalbibliothek records the series as `1723 - 35.1757 nachgewiesen`, fixing the terminal 1757 annual as no. 35.
- Independent historical/antiquarian evidence for 1755, including Katalog 37, transcribes the 1755 host as `zum drey und dreysigstenmal` (thirty-third).
- Independent evidence for 1756 identifies it as the thirty-fourth annual.
- Therefore the internally consistent sequence is:

`1755 = 33` → `1756 = 34` → `1757 = 35`.

## Evidentiary decision

Treat Kiefer's present 1755 `vier- und dreysigstenmal` wording as a **catalogue/title-transcription anomaly unless and until a photograph of that specific title page proves otherwise**.

Do not use the Kiefer 1755 ordinal to renumber the series, and do not infer an otherwise unknown skipped/duplicate issue from that catalogue wording alone.

This is also a useful bibliographic-method control: dealer title transcriptions can silently duplicate ordinal wording from an adjacent annual even when the underlying physical object is correctly dated and otherwise well collated.
