# HLQ source calibration — Schlözer painted tables, 1773/1775

Date: 2026-09-04
Status: source-level correction; article core and three-node structure unchanged

## Result

The article should distinguish the **date of Schlözer's reply to Herder** from the **edition/page currently under exact textual control**.

The response to Herder belongs to the 1773 second part of Schlözer's *Vorstellung seiner Universal-Historie*. However, the exact modern transcription presently under control for the passage on `gemahlte chronologische Tabellen` cites the **1775 second, revised edition, p. 327**. Until the 1773 institutional page is directly inspected, do not write `1773, p. 327` as though both date and page had been directly verified in the same carrier.

Safe formulation:

> In his reply to Herder, first issued in 1773, Schlözer refused to dismiss `gemahlte chronologische Tabellen`; the wording used here is controlled in the revised 1775 edition, p. 327.

This preserves the controversy chronology without overclaiming the 1773 pagination.

## 1. Painted chronological tables

Regine C. Hrosch, *Das Bild als historische Quelle?* reproduces the passage closely and cites Schlözer's revised edition. The controlled text begins:

`Über den Einfall selbst (gemahlte chronologische Tabellen) lache niemand schlechtweg ...`

and continues with the example of Solomon's Temple, the Egyptian pyramids, and Homer on one painted sheet, concluding that their `Synchronismus` can be fixed through `Association der Ideen`.

Evidence level:

`exact_secondary_transcription + revised-edition primary locator (1775, p.327)`.

The historical relation remains:

`reply to Herder first issued 1773`
→ `text presently controlled in revised 1775 edition, p.327`.

Do not collapse those two statements.

## 2. Illumination der Tabelle

The 1775 second, revised edition is bibliographically secure:

August Ludwig Schlözer, *Vorstellung der Universal-Historie*, zwote, veränderte Auflage (Göttingen: Dieterich, 1775), `[6] Bl., 307 S.`, VD18 `11056576`, ULB Sachsen-Anhalt shelfmark `AB 22 A 9/k, 26 (1)`, DOI `10.25673/95805`.

Hrosch reproduces the sentence:

`Wann man die 5 Hauptperioden auf eine in 5 Fächer als soviel Jahrtausende verteilte Tabelle bringt; so kan man die Länge und Dauer jeder Hauptperiode, durch Illumination der Tabelle, dem jungen Auge sichtlich machen.`

Evidence level:

`exact_secondary_transcription + edition secure; exact primary page unresolved`.

Do not invent a page number. Figure 1C remains pending until the institutional page is captured.

## 3. Article consequence

Figure 1 should now be described as:
