→ `rapid retrospective into Nürnberg print / engraving / heraldic information practice`

The article does **not** require a fully demonstrated causal influence chain from Nürnberg to Göttingen. It will distinguish direct personnel/publisher/material bridges from structural comparison and keep the latter explicitly non-genealogical.

## Initial governing question

How did arranging, compressing, decoding, updating, and visually displaying historical information become part of what eighteenth-century historians could call method?

Colour is retained as a high-sensitivity tracer rather than the article's sole subject. The key issue is where visible/graphic differentiation enters the historical knowledge chain and what operation it is entitled to perform.

## Provisional three-node architecture

1. **Göttingen, c. 1766–1773/75** — Gatterer, Schlözer, Universalgeschichte, Plan, Zusammenhang, Evidenz, tables, teaching, priority and the 1773 dispute.
2. **Nürnberg retrospective, c. 1723–1759 with selective earlier controls** — Köhler/Weigel, Wappenkalender, heraldic tincture encoding, colour keys, prose blazon, hand-colouring, serial updating, reusable plate stock; earlier Siebmacher/Spener only where they sharpen the technical problem.
3. **Return to Göttingen** — reread the controversy as a dispute over the legitimate material operations by which facts become an inspectable historical whole, without collapsing media into evidence or asserting unproved transfer.
