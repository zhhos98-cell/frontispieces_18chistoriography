# Anton Günther Winckelmann and the 1742 Münster/Lüdinghausen death record: identity guardrail

Date: 2026-08-28  
Status: IDENTITY UNRESOLVED — THE 1742 WESTPHALIAN DEATH/FOUNDATION RECORDS NAME AN ANTON GÜNTER WINCKELMANN, BUT THE CURRENT SWEEP DOES NOT ESTABLISH THAT THIS CLERIC IS THE SAME PERSON AS JOHANN JUST WINCKELMANN'S JURIST SON AND 1704 MANUSCRIPT CUSTODIAN.

## Result

A potentially attractive but currently unsafe identity equation has been detected and must be blocked.

The Internet-Portal `Westfälische Geschichte` indexes a June 1742 archival record in which Johann Melchior Neuhaus, chaplain at St. Martini in Münster, certifies that:

`Anton Günter Winckelmann`

- died on **5 June 1742**;
- was buried on **7 June 1742**.

A second document dated 27 June 1742 in the Generalvikariat Münster records a foundation made by the late:

`Anton Gunther Winckelmann, Vikar in Lüdinghausen`,

namely the Vikarie St. Antonii de Padua in the parish church at Lüdinghausen, endowed with 2500 Reichstaler in obligations.

Source:

- Internet-Portal `Westfälische Geschichte`, 1742 regesta, Münster / Generalvikariat records.

This cannot yet be equated with the Anton Günther Winckelmann who appears in the repository's heir-manuscript transmission chain.

## 1. Controlled identity of Johann Just Winckelmann's son

The son-side control currently rests on several independent sources.

### 1693 — Halle / Thomasius

An `Anton Günther Winckelmann` appears among the defendants/respondents in Christian Thomasius's Halle juridical-philosophical disputation environment.

Modern Thomasius correspondence scholarship specifically identifies him as respondent on 2 November 1693 for the question:

`Qvid sit accidens Praedicamentale? Qvid attributum & modus Cartesianorum?`

Bibliographic records place him among candidates in a university juridical context.

### 1702 — Leibniz

Leibniz refers to the late Johann Just Winckelmann's `son fils`, who had come to Hanover concerning his father's affairs and advanced very high claims.

### 1704 — Gerhard von Mastricht

Mastricht explicitly reports that the late historiographer's son held `varia Manuscripta` of his father and asks Leibniz to contact him.

The critical editors identify this son as:

`Anton Günther Winkelmann / Winckelmann`.

### Authority control

Modern authority data identifies an `Anton Günther Winckelmann`, GND `122952014`, occupation `Jurist`.

Thus the currently controlled son profile is:

`UNIVERSITY/JURIDICAL TRAINING`
→ `SON OF JOHANN JUST WINCKELMANN`
→ `POSTHUMOUS FAMILY/ESTATE NEGOTIATION`

## 5. Why the guardrail matters methodologically

The repository repeatedly separates stable names/titles from material identity.

The same discipline applies to persons:

`NAME IDENTITY`
≠ `PERSON IDENTITY`
≠ `ROLE IDENTITY`
≠ `ESTATE / MANUSCRIPT CUSTODY IDENTITY`.

A same-name death record must therefore not be promoted into the manuscript-transmission stemma merely because its date would neatly fill an existing chronological gap.
