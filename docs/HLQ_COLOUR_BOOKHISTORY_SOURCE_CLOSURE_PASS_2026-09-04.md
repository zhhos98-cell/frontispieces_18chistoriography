## 3. Gatterer Synopsis — copy-state evidence can be made bibliographically specific

Primary work:

Johann Christoph Gatterer, *Synopsis historiae universalis* (Göttingen, 1766; second edition 1769).

The 1766 title directly promises:

`sex tabulis, quarum duae in aes incisae coloribusque illustratae sunt`.

ULB Sachsen-Anhalt carrier:

DOI `10.25673/55581`, VD18 `11228814`, shelfmark `Na 1338` / Araújo records `Ha 179 IZEA / Na 1338`.

André de Melo Araújo's 2015 copy census explicitly separates pigment states.

### Coloured 1766 witnesses recorded by Araújo

- ULB Sachsen-Anhalt: `Ha 179 IZEA / Na 1338`;
- Gottfried Wilhelm Leibniz Bibliothek / Niedersächsische Landesbibliothek Hannover: `F-A 10010`;
- Universitätsbibliothek Tübingen: `Fn 28.2`.

### Crucial mixed state

Tübingen `Fn 28.2`:

- table I of the relevant pair is coloured;
- table II of the same edition/copy was not supplied with colour.

This is the highest-value object for Figure 2 because one copy contains both states.

### Completely uncoloured 1766 witnesses recorded by Araújo

- Universitätsbibliothek Leipzig: `Allg.Gesch.73`;
- Forschungsbibliothek Gotha: `SPB 2° 1270.00157`.

### Completely uncoloured 1769 witnesses recorded by Araújo

- Universitätsbibliothek Leipzig: `Allg.Gesch.74`;
- Landesbibliothek Oldenburg: `GE III 1 A 60`;
- Universitätsbibliothek Rostock: `Rb-930`.

Article consequence:

Do not write merely `some copies are coloured and some are not`. Name at least the Tübingen mixed witness and one wholly uncoloured witness in the note. Figure 2 should preferentially use Tübingen `Fn 28.2` so the comparison does not depend on two different institutions or editions.

## 5. Gatterer 1771 historical education — claim remains useful but image is not yet primary-closed

Primary work:

Gatterer, `Von der historischen Erziehung`, preface to *Fortsetzung der Algemeinen Welthistorie*, vol. 35 (Halle: Johann Justinus Gebauer, 1771), opening 8 pp.

Bibliographic control confirms the 35th volume and the eight-page preface.

Modern historiography securely places the essay in Gatterer's attempt to make genealogy, heraldry and numismatics into active/game-like pedagogical practices. Repository work based on the modern critical edition records colouring coats of arms and drawing/colouring family trees among the exercises, alongside coin cataloguing and material coin copies.

Evidence status for the literal colouring wording:

`critical_edition_control; direct_first_edition_page_pending`

Article use:

Keep in the return section. Do not quote diplomatically or use as a figure until the first-edition page is captured.
