## 1. The downstream shift: persistence becomes a category gate

Blumenbach's 1797 fifth edition states that, in the stricter sense, a `Rasse` is a character produced through `Degeneration` that is **unavoidably and necessarily inherited through reproduction**. By contrast, a `Spielart` does not have this necessary reproductive consequence.

The same note explicitly credits Kant's 1788 formulation of the distinction and directs the reader to Girtanner's 1796 *Über das Kantische Princip für die Naturgeschichte* for a fuller treatment.

This creates a clear threshold:

`visible difference → test reproductive persistence → category jurisdiction`

rather than:

`visible difference → race category`.

The downstream rule is therefore:

`necessary reproductive persistence = admission criterion for race-level classification`.

## 2. Persistence is not merely another trait

In this framework, inheritance is not one observed feature among others. It is a **meta-property of the character** that decides what classificatory work the character may perform.

A colour difference may be:
- perceptually real;
- causally produced;
- repeatedly observable;
- even geographically patterned;

and still fail to qualify as a race-character if it does not reproduce with the required necessity.

Thus:

`visibility ≠ persistence ≠ classificatory jurisdiction`.

This sharpens the project's earlier `inference jurisdiction` model. A character can be fully admissible at the descriptive level while barred from a more durable genealogical/classificatory category.

## 3. Girtanner as formalizer of the persistence test

Modern page-specific scholarship consistently reconstructs Girtanner's 1796 argument around the Kantian principle that, when two races of one stem mix, the character of each necessarily appears in the offspring. One recent historical treatment cites Girtanner p.39 for this organizing principle; other scholarship locates his five-race division at p.59.

A separate historical study summarizes Girtanner's treatment of climatic skin-colour change as `Schminke`: climate can alter appearance, but the resulting colour modification is not inherited and therefore does not constitute a race-transforming process.

## 4. A new distinction: causal efficacy versus historical memory

This downstream framework permits an important analytical distinction:

`cause can change appearance` ≠ `cause can write descent into the body`.

The first concerns causal efficacy.
The second concerns durable hereditary memory.

A temporary or non-necessarily inherited climatic effect may be causally genuine but epistemically shallow with respect to race classification. Conversely, a character treated as necessarily inherited acquires historical depth because reproduction is imagined to carry it forward.

## 5. Relation to Meiners 1792

The contrast with the current reconstruction of Meiners 1792 is methodologically useful.

### Meiners 1792, provisional derivative reconstruction

`plural causes → same visible outcome may have several histories → triangulate colour with other traits/provenance/reports → infer cause/descent`

This produces:
- causal underdetermination;
- phenotypic equifinality;
- cross-trait weighting;
- a plural causal field in which environment, descent, mixture and physiology remain active.

### Girtanner / Blumenbach 1796–97 downstream formalization

`visible character → is it necessarily inherited? → yes/no → race versus non-race jurisdiction`

This is a stronger categorical gate. It reduces ambiguity not necessarily by measuring colour more precisely, but by changing the **temporal condition under which a visible difference is allowed to count historically**.

Do not infer direct transition from Meiners to Girtanner without transfer evidence. Use the contrast as a downstream control on the changing architecture of evidence.

## 6. Wilson-informed consequence

The Wilson-informed question can now be sharpened from:

> How does a visible difference acquire temporal depth sufficient to become historical evidence?

into:

> **What operation converts persistence itself into evidence?**

In the Girtanner/Blumenbach control, reproduction performs that operation. Reproductive recurrence turns an appearance into a durable class-character.

This gives a new chain:

`visible difference → recurrence across generation → necessary inheritance claim → historical memory → classificatory jurisdiction`.

The crucial point is that this is not a neutral discovery of persistence. It requires:
- a breeding/admixture model;
- claims about lineage continuity;
- decisions about which traits count as recurrent;
- exclusion of anomalies and temporary/acquired changes;
- an inference from observed or reported generations to necessity.

Thus the `persistence gate` itself has evidentiary prerequisites.

## 7. Falsification questions

Before promoting this into a general late-Enlightenment rule, test:

1. Does Girtanner explicitly call climatic colour change `Schminke`, and on what page/context?
2. Does he state directly that such colour change is not inherited, or is this a later reconstruction from his theory?
3. Which traits, besides colour, does he treat as necessarily inherited race-characters?
4. How much generational evidence does he actually require before moving from recurrence to `unausbleiblich` / necessary inheritance?
5. Does Blumenbach's 1797 wording reproduce Girtanner, Kant, or Blumenbach's own synthesis?
6. Does the persistence gate alter only nomenclature (`Rasse` vs `Spielart`) or also the causal narratives allowed for human history?

## 8. Current strong formulation

> The 1796–97 Girtanner/Blumenbach control shows a downstream reorganization of visible human difference in which reproductive persistence becomes a jurisdictional gate. A bodily character does not qualify for race-level historical classification merely because it is visible, repeated or causally intelligible; it must be construed as necessarily transmissible through generation. The epistemic threshold therefore shifts from identifying the cause of an appearance to establishing the temporal persistence by which appearance is imagined to carry descent forward.

This should remain a downstream comparator until direct transfer to Meiners or another pre-1792 actor is demonstrated.
