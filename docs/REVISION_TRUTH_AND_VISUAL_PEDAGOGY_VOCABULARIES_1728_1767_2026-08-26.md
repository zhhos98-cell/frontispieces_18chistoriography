# Revision, historical truth, and visual pedagogy — three contemporary registers, 1728–1767

## Status

**The earlier research lead should be corrected. Contemporary evidence strongly supports three adjacent vocabularies—serial revision, heraldic discovery of historical truth, and plate-based pedagogy—but the present pass does not verify a period advertisement/review literally calling the auxiliary sciences instruments for `Prüfung der Wahrheit`. Do not merge these registers into a quotation that has not been located.**

## 1. Serial/commercial register: `neu vermehrt und verbessert`

The Weigel/Köhler annual does not merely acquire `improved` language in occasional bookseller advertising. The formula is embedded in the formal annual title across decades.

Physical-copy catalogues preserve, among others:

- 1728: `zum sechstenmahl neu-vermehrter und verbesserter Geschichts-, Geschlechts- und Wappen-Calender`;
- 1732: `zum zehendenmahl neu-vermehrter und verbesserter...`;
- 1737: `zum funffzehendenmal neu vermehrter und verbesserter...`;
- 1744: `zum zwey und zwantzigstenmahl neu vermehrter und verbesserter...`;
- 1751: `zum neun und zwanzigstenmal neu vermehrter und verbesserter...`;
- 1755: `zum drey und dreysigstenmal neu vermehrter und verbesserter...`;
- 1757: `zum fünf und dreysigstenmal neu vermehrter und verbesserter...`.

The formula therefore belongs to the identity of the serial itself. The annual promises continuity and revision simultaneously.

This is important for a corpus in which plates and genealogical tables are physically carried forward while historical-medal modules, reigning dynasties, portraits and armorial data are updated.

### Safe interpretation

`neu vermehrt / verbessert` should be treated as a **serial maintenance and revision claim**, not automatically as a claim of critical historical truth.

## 2. 1767 bookseller advertisement: revision becomes explicit reinspection

A primary newspaper advertisement in the *Schlesische privilegirte Zeitungen* (1767, p.629 in the surviving issue) offers:

`Fortgesetzter Wappen-Calender auf das Jahr 1767, oder jährliches Handbuch der neuesten Genealogie und Heraldik, aufs neue durchgesehen, verbessert und vermehrt von Joh. Fried. Seyfert`.

The wording is stronger than generic `new edition` language:

- `aufs neue durchgesehen` — re-inspected / reviewed anew;
- `verbessert` — corrected/improved;
- `vermehrt` — augmented.

The commercial identity of the post-Gatterer/Seyfart serial therefore explicitly foregrounds **reinspection + correction + accumulation**.

This sits well with the material transformation already reconstructed from Bernd 1830, but the two sources operate at different levels:

- 1767 advertisement = contemporaneous market language;
- Bernd 1830 = retrospective specialist explanation of why the serial/cumulative armorial structure changed.

## 3. Heraldic epistemic register: Köhler 1749 and `historische Wahrheit`

Johann David Köhler's 1749 work carries the programmatic title:

`Gezeigter und bestärckter Nutz der Wappenkenntnüß zur Entdeckung einer historischen Wahrheit...`

The case asks which Duke Heinrich of Lüneburg should be associated with a silver ship vowed at Altötting in 1518. Bibliographic controls further give the physical apparatus:

`Nebst einer in Kupffer gestochenen Abbildung desselben auf zwo Tafeln`.

Thus the title itself joins:

`Wappenkenntnüß`
→ `Nutz`
→ `Entdeckung einer historischen Wahrheit`

inside an argument materially equipped with two engraved representations of the disputed object.

This is a much stronger contemporary bridge between heraldic knowledge, material/graphic evidence, and historical truth than an unlocated generic phrase such as `Prüfung der Wahrheit`.

### Guard

The title says `Entdeckung einer historischen Wahrheit`, not that all heraldry is a general-purpose `Prüfung der Wahrheit`. Preserve the specificity of the claim.

## 4. Visual-pedagogical register: Gatterer 1766/1767

Gatterer's 1766 *Abriß der Heraldik oder Wappenkunde* is formally presented as:

`zum Nutzen der studirenden Jugend entworfen und mit 8 Kupfertafeln erläutert`.

The table of contents begins with `Erklärung und Eintheilung der Wappen` and includes a dedicated section `Quellen der Wappenkunde` (from p.7).

The same 1767 newspaper advertisement that advertises the revised Seyfart annual immediately lists Gatterer's manual:

`Abriß der Heraldik der Wappenkunde, zum Nutzen der studirenden Jugend entworfen, und mit 8 Kupfertafeln erläutert`.

This provides a clean contemporaneous juxtaposition:

`annual latest genealogy/heraldry → re-inspected, corrected, augmented`

beside

`heraldic manual for students → explained through eight copperplates`.

The market is selling two different operations, not one:

- maintenance/update of a serial information apparatus;
- visualized instruction in the discipline.

## 5. Three registers should remain analytically distinct

The evidence now supports:

### A. Serial revision
`neu vermehrt` / `verbessert` / `aufs neue durchgesehen`

### B. Historical truth discovery
Köhler 1749: `Nutz der Wappenkenntnüß zur Entdeckung einer historischen Wahrheit`

### C. Visual pedagogy
Gatterer 1766: `zum Nutzen der studirenden Jugend ... mit 8 Kupfertafeln erläutert`

They can be related historically because they inhabit the same genealogy/heraldry publishing ecology. They should not be collapsed into a single contemporary slogan.

## 6. Correction to old research lead 018

The old lead stated that research drafts cited both:

- advertising language `neu vermehrt und verbessert`;
- reviews calling auxiliary sciences instruments for `Prüfung der Wahrheit`.

After the present pass:

- the first is **verified and stronger than expected**, because it is sustained title language and survives into a 1767 primary newspaper advertisement;
- the second remains **unverified as a literal period quotation**.

Until an exact source is found, write instead:

> Contemporary print culture separately framed the annual apparatus as repeatedly reviewed/corrected/augmented, Köhler's heraldic case as a means of discovering a historical truth, and Gatterer's heraldic textbook as copperplate-supported instruction.

Do not write:

> Contemporary reviews called the auxiliary sciences `instruments for Prüfung der Wahrheit`.

## 7. Relation to the Clio inscription

This separation actually strengthens interpretation of `TESTES TEMPORUM ET VERITATIS`.

The image need not be glossed by finding a later prose sentence that repeats its words exactly. The surrounding print ecology shows distinct operations through which witness authority was enacted:

- annual correction and augmentation;
- heraldic identification of a disputed historical object/person;
- copperplate-supported training;
- later, Gatterer's explicit page-adjacency `Evidenz` and original/surrogate training loops.

The inscription can therefore be situated inside an operational ecology without pretending that all these practices share one fixed lexicon.
