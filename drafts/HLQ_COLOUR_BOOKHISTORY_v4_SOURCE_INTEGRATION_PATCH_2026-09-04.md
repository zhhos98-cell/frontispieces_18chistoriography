## Patch 3 — Gatterer 1774 becomes the main colour-sign bridge

After the paragraph on the changing 1759–1766 *Handbuch* sequence, insert:

> Gatterer's expanded *Abriß der Heraldik* supplies a more explicit connection to the older colour-sign literature. On p. 12 of the second edition (Nürnberg: Raspe, 1774), he discusses two additional hatch signs attributed to Rink: one for *Naturfarbe* (fig. 19) and another for *Eisenfarbe* (fig. 20). Gatterer judged the first superfluous but the second useful because, he wrote, he had learned from old *Wappenbriefe* that heralds in Germany could use *Eisenfarbe* and *weise Farbe*, that is silver, as equivalent expressions.[^34a]
>
> The paragraph then points the reader directly backward: `Zur Erläuterung dieser Abhandlung vom Ursprunge der Schrafirungen dient das oben angeführte köhlerische Programma.` The programme can be identified as Johann David Köhler's Göttingen *Programma De Inventoribus Incisurarum Gallice Les Hachures Dictarum Quibus Metalla Et Colores In Tesseris Gentilitiis Absque Pigmentis Indicantur* of 1736.[^34b] The relation is unusually literal for this article: Köhler had published a programme on hachures by which metals and colours could be indicated without pigments; Gatterer later discussed particular colour hatchings in his own heraldic handbook and explicitly sent readers back to that programme. No claim about the transfer of a general historical method is required.

Evidence guard: the Gatterer wording is currently an exact secondary transcription with fixed primary edition/page. Bibliographic control of the 1774 second edition is direct; BSB/MDZ page-image capture remains pending.
